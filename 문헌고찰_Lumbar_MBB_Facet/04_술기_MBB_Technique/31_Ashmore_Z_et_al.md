## 이 문헌에서 인용된 근거 (1건)

### [확실] Meta-analysis is LESS favorable: pooled 11% absolute risk difference of INCORRECT needle placement for US-guided MBB vs fluoroscopic confirmation; certainty of evidence low to very low.

Systematic review and meta-analysis: pooled analysis of 7 studies demonstrated an 11% risk difference of incorrect needle placement for US-guided MBB confirmed using fluoroscopy with and without contrast (P < 0.0009). Pooled analysis of 3 studies demonstrated a 13% risk difference of incorrect needle placement for US-guided intra-articular facet joint injection confirmed by CT (P < 0.0001). Conclusion: 'US-guided lumbar MBB and FJI are associated with a significant risk of incorrect needle placement when confirmed by fluoroscopy or CT'; GRADE certainty low to very low. Present this alongside the 91-95% single-study accuracy figures — the deck should show both positions.
