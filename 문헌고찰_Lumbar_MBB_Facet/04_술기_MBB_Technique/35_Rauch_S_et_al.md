## 이 문헌에서 인용된 근거 (1건)

### [확실] US LIMITATION 1 — BMI. Success falls with obesity; sources DISAGREE on whether obesity is an absolute barrier.

Numbers: 'The correct target was reached in 72.5% of procedures overall, but in 80.5% of procedures on patients with BMI <30.' Patients with BMI > 35 kg/m2 are commonly EXCLUDED from US studies because of poor visualization; resolution decreases with the depth of the fat layer. DISAGREEMENT: Greher et al. found ultrasound landmarks of adequate quality even in a subject with BMI 36 kg/m2 (landmarks 'good in 19 and of sufficient quality in one' of 20 volunteers) and therefore regarded obesity as a NON-absolute contraindication; Rauch et al., in a dedicated fluoroscopically confirmed feasibility study in obese patients, concluded that medial branch blocks CANNOT reliably be performed under ultrasound guidance in obese patients. Report both.
