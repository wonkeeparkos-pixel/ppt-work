## 이 문헌에서 인용된 근거 (2건)

### [확실] US non-inferiority for clinical outcome: a multicenter RCT found US-guided LMBB non-inferior to fluoroscopy for pain and disability at 1 week and 1 month.

50 adults with lumbar facet joint syndrome randomized to fluoroscopy-guided vs ultrasound-guided blocks at three levels (L3-L4, L4-L5, L5-S1), using the needle TRANSVERSE approach in both arms. Outcomes: VAPS, Oswestry Disability Index, Duke's Activity Status Index at baseline, 1 week, 1 month. US was non-inferior (P = 0.047) across VAPS, ODI and DASI at both timepoints. Advantage cited: irradiation-free.

### [보통] Practical ultrasound workflow summary for the deck.

1) Prone, pillow under abdomen. 2) Curvilinear 2-5 MHz probe, parasagittal 3-4 cm lateral to midline; obtain the TRIDENT sign (successive TP domes with acoustic shadows); count levels from the sacrum upward, mark midpoints. 3) Rotate 90° to axial: identify spinous process, lamina, facet joint, SAP, TP. 4) Target = groove at the cephalad margin of the TP where it meets the SAP. 5) 20-22 G spinal needle (22 G 10 cm for deeper patients), IN-PLANE lateral to medial at 45-60° to skin, to bony contact. 6) Rotate probe back to longitudinal to confirm the tip lies on the cephalad aspect of the TP. 7) Inject small volume, watching for the fluid to layer against bone. 8) For L5 dorsal ramus: transducer over the S1 SAP / sacral ala; needle inserted just caudad to probe midpoint, advanced caudocephalad to the S1-ala junction; oblique OUT-OF-PLANE in a rotated cross-axis view is the validated variant; verify in longitudinal view that the tip has not passed the ala into the L5/S1 foramen. 9) Accept that intravascular uptake cannot be detected — prefer fluoroscopy for diagnostic blocks where a false negative matters.
