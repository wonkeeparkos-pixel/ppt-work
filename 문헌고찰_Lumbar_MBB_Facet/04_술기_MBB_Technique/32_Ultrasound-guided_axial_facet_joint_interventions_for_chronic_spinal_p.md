## 이 문헌에서 인용된 근거 (2건)

### [보통] Aggregated accuracy ranges: US-guided lumbar intra-articular facet injection 86-100%; US-guided medial branch block 72-97%.

'Accuracy with ultrasound-guided lumbar facet joint intra-articular injection (86%–100%) was more reliable than medial branch block (72%–97%); analgesia was comparable to fluoroscopy and CT guidance.' The ASRA evidence-based assessment concluded US guidance 'is not only feasible but also clinically useful' for cervical facet/medial branch blocks and that 'sonography is useful for medial branch blocks and intra-articular facet blocks in the cervical and lumbar spine but there is little evidence to support its use in the thoracic spine.'

*원 출처 표기*: `Ultrasound-guided axial facet joint interventions for chronic spinal pain: A narrative review, Can J Pain 2023, PMC10193888; Neal JM et al., The ASRA evidence-based medicine assessment of ultrasound-guided regional anesthesia and pain medicine: executive summary, Reg Anesth Pain Med 2010, PMID 20216019`

### [보통] US LIMITATION 3 — depth, and no ability to detect intravascular uptake in real time. A learning curve exists and has been quantified with CUSUM methodology.

'These procedures were more challenging for patients with obesity, and deeper structures were more difficult to accurately target (e.g., lower cervical levels, L5 dorsal ramus).' Ultrasound cannot demonstrate contrast flow, so the 6-20% intravascular uptake documented under fluoroscopy is undetectable — a structural argument for fluoroscopy when the block is DIAGNOSTIC (false-negative avoidance) rather than therapeutic. A dedicated study has characterized acquisition of the US MBB technique using CUSUM curves.

*원 출처 표기*: `Ultrasound-guided axial facet joint interventions: A narrative review, Can J Pain 2023, PMC10193888; Ultrasound Block of the Medial Branch: Learning the Technique Using CUSUM Curves, PMC9004262`
