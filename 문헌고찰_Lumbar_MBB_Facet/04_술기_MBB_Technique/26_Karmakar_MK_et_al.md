## 이 문헌에서 인용된 근거 (1건)

### [확실] ULTRASOUND — two-step scanning protocol: parasagittal 'trident' TP view to count levels, then rotate 90° to the transverse view to inject.

Step 1: place a 2-5 MHz low-frequency curvilinear probe in the LONGITUDINAL (parasagittal) plane 3-4 cm lateral to the midline of the spinous processes at the level to be blocked; sweep medial-lateral until successive transverse processes appear. In this parasagittal view the TPs appear as hyperechoic domes with sausage-like acoustic shadows beneath them — the classic 'TRIDENT SIGN' (named after Neptune's trident); the shadowed intervals between them are the acoustic windows. Mark the midpoint of each lumbar level. Step 2: rotate the probe 90° to the AXIAL/transverse view to display the spinous process, lamina, facet joint, SAP and TP.
