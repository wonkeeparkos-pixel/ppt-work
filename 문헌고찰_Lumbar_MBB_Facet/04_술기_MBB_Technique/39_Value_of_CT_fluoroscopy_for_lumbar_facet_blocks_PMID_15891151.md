## 이 문헌에서 인용된 근거 (1건)

### [보통] CT / CT-FLUOROSCOPY guidance: same anatomic target, used when precision or soft-tissue avoidance matters.

'The dorsal ramus of the spinal nerve gives rise to the medial branch, which courses along a notch between the superior articular process and transverse process, with the target point for medial branch blocks and radiofrequency ablation located within this notch IMMEDIATELY PROXIMAL TO THE MAMILLOACCESSORY LIGAMENT.' Rationale for CT fluoro: 'allows more-precise needle tip positioning', 'real-time visualization on cross-sectional images, resulting in a superior ability to avoid adjacent soft-tissue structures and to achieve more-precise needle placement'. Practical indications (inferred from that rationale rather than stated as a list): distorted or fused post-surgical anatomy, severe facet hypertrophy/osteophyte obscuring the notch, high iliac crest at L5-S1, obesity defeating both fluoro landmarks and ultrasound, and operators already working in a CT suite. Needle advanced in AP/oblique/lateral planes until the tip is perpendicular to the mammillary process at the vertebral body and/or sacral ala.
