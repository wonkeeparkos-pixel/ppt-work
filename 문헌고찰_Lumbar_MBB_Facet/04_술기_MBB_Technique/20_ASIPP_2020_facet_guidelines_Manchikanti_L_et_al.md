## 이 문헌에서 인용된 근거 (1건)

### [확실] STEROID SHOULD NOT be added to a DIAGNOSTIC medial branch block — local anesthetic only.

2024 Consensus Guidelines: 'Diagnostic medial branch blocks are to be performed with local anesthetic agents only. The concurrent use of steroids is not medically necessary, as it may compromise the integrity of the diagnostic test.' The mechanistic argument: a diagnostic block is interpreted against the expected pharmacologic duration of the anesthetic; adding steroid produces relief extending far beyond that window, so the temporal signature that defines a true-positive is destroyed, and any anti-inflammatory effect on structures other than the medial branch confounds attribution. Steroid belongs to the therapeutic block, after the diagnosis is made.
