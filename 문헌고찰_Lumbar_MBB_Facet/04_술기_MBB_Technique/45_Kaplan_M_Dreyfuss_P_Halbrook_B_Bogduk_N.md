## 이 문헌에서 인용된 근거 (1건)

### [확실] Physiologic ceiling: even a technically perfect MBB fails to anesthetize the joint in ~11% of subjects — aberrant innervation.

18 asymptomatic volunteers randomized to L4-L5 or L5-S1 zygapophysial joint injection with contrast until capsular distension elicited pain WITHOUT extracapsular spread. One week later, 15 blinded subjects received randomized saline or 2% lidocaine medial branch injections matching the innervation of the previously distended joint. Of 9 who received 2% lidocaine MBBs, 8 felt no pain on repeat capsular distension and 1 still felt pain. There was a significant effect of 2% lidocaine vs saline on anesthetizing the joint WHEN VENOUS UPTAKE WAS AVOIDED. Interpretation commonly quoted: ~11% of individuals continue to experience pain from capsular distension despite MBB, suggesting aberrant innervation — an irreducible false-negative floor.
