## 이 문헌에서 인용된 근거 (1건)

### [보통] Even live fluoroscopy misses intravascular uptake — digital subtraction (DSA) detected an additional 27/344 blocks (a ~71% relative increase over live fluoro alone).

Live fluoroscopy revealed inadvertent vascular uptake in 38 of 344 blocks = 11% (95% CI 8.0–15%). Digital subtraction technology uncovered an additional 27 of 344 blocks with vascular uptake NOT detected with conventional live fluoroscopy (i.e., 65/344 total ≈ 19% by DSA vs 11% by live fluoro). This reframes live fluoro as better-than-aspiration but still imperfect.
