### [확실] US validation, cadaver/CT gold standard: 94% simulated block success (Greher).

CT-confirmed accuracy study: 45 of 50 needle tips were located at the EXACT target point, with the remaining 5 within 5 mm of target. In 47 of 50 cases (94%) the applied contrast dye reached the groove where the nerve lies — a simulated block success rate of 94%.
