## 이 문헌에서 인용된 근거 (1건)

### [확실] Guideline status: diagnostic facet joint nerve blocks carry Level II evidence with moderate strength of recommendation; double-block technique with an 80% improvement threshold is the standard for predicting RFA response.

ASIPP 2020: 'Diagnostic medial nerve blocks by the double-injection technique with an 80% improvement threshold are an option to predict a favorable response to medial nerve ablation for facet-mediated chronic lower-back pain without radiculopathy'; 'Overall, evidence was Level II for diagnostic facet joint nerve blocks with moderate strength of recommendations in lumbar, cervical, and thoracic spine.' Note the threshold disagreement in the literature: Schwarzer et al. defined a positive block as ≥50% pain reduction, whereas ASIPP uses ≥80% relief with restoration of movements that normally cause pain. The 2020 multispecialty consensus (17 questions, 100% committee consensus) concluded MBB is more predictive of RFA success than intra-articular injection.
