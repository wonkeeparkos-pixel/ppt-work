## 이 문헌에서 인용된 근거 (1건)

### [보통] Number of levels per session in typical guideline/payer practice: a maximum of 2 levels (unilateral or bilateral) per diagnostic session.

'For each covered spinal region (cervical or lumbar), diagnostic medial branch blocks may be performed at a maximum of two (2) levels, either unilateral or bilateral, per session.' Note this is a coverage/consensus constraint rather than an anatomic one — and it interacts awkwardly with the 3-nerves-for-2-joints anatomy (e.g., L3 MB + L4 MB + L5 dorsal ramus to cover L4-5 and L5-S1). Flag for the deck: 'levels' in payer language usually means facet joint levels, not nerves.
