## 이 문헌에서 인용된 근거 (1건)

### [확실] Local anesthetic choice: lidocaine 1-2% and bupivacaine 0.25-0.5% are the standard agents for comparative blocks — but new data undermine the 'concordant duration' requirement.

Classic comparative protocol: first block with 1% (or 2%) lidocaine, confirmatory block with 0.25% (or 0.5%) bupivacaine, requiring longer relief with the longer-acting agent. A multicenter study of patients achieving ≥80% relief found NO statistically significant difference in duration between agents, and the direction was paradoxical on one metric: by NRS, mean duration of ≥80% relief was 3.5 h with bupivacaine vs 16.4 h with lidocaine; by percentage-pain-relief method, 19.2 h bupivacaine vs 12.2 h lidocaine. Authors conclude 'any emphasis on concordant duration of relief from specific anesthetics utilized for diagnostic medial branch blocks should be reconsidered.'
