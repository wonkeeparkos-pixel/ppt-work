## 이 문헌에서 인용된 근거 (1건)

### [확실] Principal technical FALSE-NEGATIVE mechanism is intravascular uptake — the injectate is washed away before it reaches the nerve.

'Intravascular uptake of injectate causes a false-negative response to block and is associated with various possible complications.' Given the 6.1-20% incidence depending on detection method and the 34% sensitivity of aspiration, a block done without live-fluoro contrast will silently generate false negatives in roughly 1 in 10 nerves. Other false-negative mechanisms: needle tip too superficial / not in the notch (the 5/95 'lamina placements' seen in the US validation study are the sonographic analogue), wrong segment blocked (0/101 in Shim's US series but 3/95 in the Asian Spine J series), and aberrant innervation.
