### [보통] LATERAL view is the depth/safety check — used specifically to exclude foraminal (ventral) advancement.

'If there is concern about depth or inadvertent entry into the foramen, then obtain a lateral view.' The needle tip must point INTO the notch between SAP and TP and must not be advanced transforaminally. Cadaveric work has documented anterior spread through the intervertebral foramen with injectate reaching as far anterior as the lateral aspect of the vertebral body when the needle/injectate is mispositioned. Practical anti-slip technique (widely taught but not traceable to a single primary citation): aim slightly medially so first bony contact is the SAP/lamina, then walk the needle laterally/caudally off the SAP into the notch — this prevents the bevel from sliding ventrally off the superior TP surface into psoas or the foramen.
