## 이 문헌에서 인용된 근거 (4건)

### [확실] L5 DORSAL RAMUS block: target is the junction of the S1 superior articular process and the sacral ala — specifically the middle of the base of the S1 SAP, just below the sacral ala.

At L5 the dorsal ramus itself (not a discrete medial branch) is blocked, because the L5 medial branch arises from the dorsal ramus distal to this point. Target: 'The L5 dorsal ramus is at the junction of the S1 superior articular process (SAP) and sacral ala. The L5 dorsal ramus target point is located at the middle of the base of the superior articular process, and therefore, slightly below the sacral ala.' Skin wheal and soft tissues over the L5-S1 region between the L5 SAP and the sacral ala with 1% lidocaine via 25-G needle; a 25-G spinal needle is then advanced to the S1 SAP/ala junction.

*원 출처 표기*: `Musculoskeletal Key / Radiology Key, Lumbar Zygapophysial Joint Nerve (Medial Branch) Injection—Oblique Approach; ASRA Pain Medicine blog, Ultrasound-Guided Lumbar Medial Branch and Intra-Articular Facet Injections (2019)`

### [확실] ULTRASOUND — two-step scanning protocol: parasagittal 'trident' TP view to count levels, then rotate 90° to the transverse view to inject.

Step 1: place a 2-5 MHz low-frequency curvilinear probe in the LONGITUDINAL (parasagittal) plane 3-4 cm lateral to the midline of the spinous processes at the level to be blocked; sweep medial-lateral until successive transverse processes appear. In this parasagittal view the TPs appear as hyperechoic domes with sausage-like acoustic shadows beneath them — the classic 'TRIDENT SIGN' (named after Neptune's trident); the shadowed intervals between them are the acoustic windows. Mark the midpoint of each lumbar level. Step 2: rotate the probe 90° to the AXIAL/transverse view to display the spinous process, lamina, facet joint, SAP and TP.

*원 출처 표기*: `ASRA Pain Medicine blog, Ultrasound-Guided Lumbar Medial Branch and Intra-Articular Facet Injections (2019); NYSORA, Sonography of the Lumbar Paravertebral Space; Karmakar MK et al., sonoanatomy for paramedian neuraxial blocks, PMID 22010025`

### [확실] US target and needling: transverse view, IN-PLANE lateral-to-medial, 45-60° to skin, 20-22 G spinal needle, target = the groove at the cephalad margin of the TP adjacent to the SAP; then rotate back to longitudinal to confirm.

'The target point is the groove at the cephalad margin of the transverse process adjacent to the superior articular process.' Under real-time guidance with an in-plane technique a 20-22 G spinal needle is inserted lateral to medial at approximately 45°-60° to the skin (a 22-G 10 cm Quincke is specified in one protocol for the axial in-plane approach). After bony contact, rotate the probe 90° back to the longitudinal view to verify the tip sits on the cephalad aspect of the TP — the two-plane check substitutes for the fluoroscopic AP/lateral pair. Greher's original description used a paravertebral CROSS-AXIS view and a LONG-AXIS view defined at 15 MHz, with block needles guided at 2-6 MHz.

*원 출처 표기*: `ASRA Pain Medicine blog (2019); Greher M et al., Ultrasound-guided lumbar facet nerve block: a sonoanatomic study of a new methodologic approach, Anesthesiology 2004;100:1242-1248, PMID 15114223`

### [확실] US LIMITATION 2 — the L5 dorsal ramus is the hardest target; a dedicated oblique out-of-plane technique achieves only 80% in unselected cadavers (100% if no spondylolisthesis).

20 US-guided L5 dorsal ramus approaches using a NEW OBLIQUE OUT-OF-PLANE technique in a ROTATED CROSS-AXIS view, bilaterally in 10 cadavers: 16 attempts at the exact radiological target, 1 slightly too lateral, 3 slightly too caudal (3-10 mm away) — overall success 80% in unpreselected cadavers, but 100% in the subgroup WITHOUT spondylolisthesis. Practical US technique: the transducer is positioned over the S1 SAP and sacral ala; the needle is inserted immediately caudad to the midpoint of the transducer and advanced caudocephalad until the tip contacts the S1/sacral ala junction; a longitudinal view then verifies the tip has not passed beyond the sacral ala into the L5/S1 foramen. If the iliac crest obscures the window, use out-of-plane, or in-plane with an oblique tilt of the transversely oriented probe.

*원 출처 표기*: `Greher M, Moriggl B, Peng PWH, Minella CE, Zacchino M, Eichenberger U. Ultrasound-Guided Approach for L5 Dorsal Ramus Block and Fluoroscopic Evaluation in Unpreselected Cadavers, Reg Anesth Pain Med 2015;40(6):713-717, PMID 26414871; ASRA Pain Medicine blog (2019)`
