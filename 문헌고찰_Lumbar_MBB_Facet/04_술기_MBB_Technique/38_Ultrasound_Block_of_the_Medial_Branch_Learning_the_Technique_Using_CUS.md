## 이 문헌에서 인용된 근거 (1건)

### [보통] US LIMITATION 3 — depth, and no ability to detect intravascular uptake in real time. A learning curve exists and has been quantified with CUSUM methodology.

'These procedures were more challenging for patients with obesity, and deeper structures were more difficult to accurately target (e.g., lower cervical levels, L5 dorsal ramus).' Ultrasound cannot demonstrate contrast flow, so the 6-20% intravascular uptake documented under fluoroscopy is undetectable — a structural argument for fluoroscopy when the block is DIAGNOSTIC (false-negative avoidance) rather than therapeutic. A dedicated study has characterized acquisition of the US MBB technique using CUSUM curves.
