### [미확인] Sedation during diagnostic blocks is a selection/validity hazard, not merely a comfort question.

UNVERIFIED IN-SESSION. Both SIS and ASIPP discourage meaningful sedation for DIAGNOSTIC blocks on the grounds that anxiolysis and analgesia inflate the false-positive rate by producing non-specific pain relief. Manchikanti's group published specifically on this (Pain Physician, mid-2000s, evaluating the effect of sedation on diagnostic validity of facet joint nerve blocks) — I could not verify the citation and would present the principle without attributing a number. If sedation is unavoidable for tolerability, the block result must be interpreted with that caveat documented.
