## 이 문헌에서 인용된 근거 (2건)

### [보통] Clinical predictors that FAILED validation — the honest core of patient selection, and the strongest evidence in this whole topic.

UNVERIFIED IN-SESSION but these are landmark negative studies I recall with reasonable specificity, and the parent's existing content_mbb.py already carries several with the same numbers, which is corroborating. (a) Jackson RP et al., Spine 1988 — 390 patients, 127 clinical variables analyzed; NO clinical finding predicted block response. (b) Schwarzer AC et al., Spine 1994;19(10):1132-1137, 'Clinical features of patients with pain stemming from the lumbar zygapophysial joints. Is the lumbar facet syndrome a clinical entity?' — tested the pre-existing Fairbank (Spine 1981, classic) and Helbig & Lee (Spine 1988;13(1):61-64) facet-syndrome criteria against controlled blocks; both failed. (c) Schwarzer AC et al., Ann Rheum Dis 1995;54(2):100-106 (PMID recalled 7702395) — prevalence 40% (95% CI 27-53) in an Australian chronic LBP population, with the conclusion that no historical or examination feature distinguished responders. (d) Revel M et al., Spine 1998;23(18):1972-1976 (PMID recalled 9779530) — 80 patients, the ONLY positive model: 5 of 7 criteria gave sensitivity 92%, specificity 80%. (e) Laslett M et al., BMC Musculoskelet Disord 2004 — external validation of Revel's model failed badly, sensitivity <17% (specificity ~90-93%), concluding it is unsuitable as a screening test; Laslett also published clinical prediction rule work in Spine J 2006. (f) Maas ET et al., Eur J Pain 2017;21(3):403-414 (PMID recalled 27723170) — systematic review of history and physical examination: evidence insufficient; history and exam cannot obviate diagnostic blocks.

### [보통] Revel's criteria are widely mis-taught: they select for pain that is NOT mechanically provoked — the opposite of the 'worse with extension/rotation' folklore.

UNVERIFIED IN-SESSION. The 7 recalled Revel items are: age >65 years; pain not exacerbated by coughing; not worsened by forward flexion; not worsened by rising from flexion; not worsened by hyperextension; not worsened by extension-rotation; and well relieved by recumbency. Five of seven positive gave sens 92% / spec 80% in the derivation set. Two teaching points the audience will not expect: (1) the model contains NO referred-pain-pattern item at all; (2) five of the seven items are NEGATIVES of provocation — so the classic bedside teaching 'facet pain is worse with extension and rotation' is not merely unvalidated, it is scored in the opposite direction by the only model that ever performed well. And that model failed external validation (Laslett 2004, sensitivity <17%). The parent's content_mbb.py already makes this point in Korean; it is worth keeping.
