## 이 문헌에서 인용된 근거 (1건)

### [확실] The single organizing principle across ALL bodies (ASIPP, SIS, Cohen 2020 multispecialty, NASS, Medicare): there is no clinical, physical-exam, or imaging finding that establishes lumbar facet-mediated pain — the diagnosis rests exclusively on response to controlled anesthetic blocks.

UNVERIFIED IN-SESSION. This is the one statement I would put on a slide with confidence, because it is concordant across every guideline body and every negative validation study, and it is the logical premise for why diagnostic MBB exists at all. Corollaries that follow from it and are also concordant: (a) facet osteoarthritis on CT/MRI does not select responders and is not an indication; (b) imaging's role in the indication set is to EXCLUDE alternative/red-flag pathology, not to confirm facet pain; (c) a 'facet syndrome' checklist cannot substitute for a block. Practical framing for the deck: the block is the diagnostic test, and the history/exam only gates who is worth testing.
