### [보통] Absolute contraindications to lumbar MBB / facet injection.

UNVERIFIED IN-SESSION; this is standard interventional-pain practice consensus rather than a numbered guideline recommendation, and is uncontroversial. (1) Patient refusal or inability to consent. (2) Active infection at the planned needle entry site (cellulitis, abscess) or systemic infection/bacteremia — facet septic arthritis, epidural abscess, and discitis are the feared sequelae. (3) Uncorrected coagulopathy or clinically significant thrombocytopenia — distinct from therapeutic anticoagulation, which is a relative issue and largely permissive for low-risk facet procedures per ASRA 2018. (4) True documented anaphylaxis to an essential injectate component (local anesthetic, corticosteroid preparation, or contrast) where no substitution is possible. Note that most reported 'local anesthetic allergy' is not IgE-mediated and often reflects preservative (methylparaben) or ester-class sensitivity — an amide agent is usually usable after allergy evaluation. (5) Some authorities add unstable/acutely decompensated medical illness in which the procedure cannot be safely performed.
