### [미확인] Reference holding intervals (from the ASRA neuraxial 4th edition) — applicable to intermediate/high-risk procedures, NOT required for low-risk facet work, but useful as the comparison column on a slide.

UNVERIFIED IN-SESSION and recalled from the neuraxial guideline, so directionally standard but check each row: aspirin — no hold; clopidogrel — 5-7 days; prasugrel — 7-10 days; ticagrelor — 5-7 days; warfarin — hold until INR normalized (commonly stated as <=1.4); rivaroxaban — 3 days; apixaban — 3 days; dabigatran — 4-5 days, longer with reduced creatinine clearance; fondaparinux — 4 days; LMWH prophylactic dose — 12 hours; LMWH therapeutic dose — 24 hours. The pedagogical value is the contrast: these are the intervals you do NOT need to impose for a low-risk facet MBB, which is why MBB is often the procedure of choice in the anticoagulated axial-pain patient.
