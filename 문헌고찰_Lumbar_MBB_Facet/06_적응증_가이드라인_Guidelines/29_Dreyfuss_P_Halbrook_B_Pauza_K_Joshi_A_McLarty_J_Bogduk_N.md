### [보통] Dreyfuss 2000 is the benchmark for what RFA achieves when patients are selected by rigorous dual blocks with an 80% threshold.

UNVERIFIED IN-SESSION. Dreyfuss P, Halbrook B, Pauza K, Joshi A, McLarty J, Bogduk N. 'Efficacy and validity of radiofrequency neurotomy for chronic lumbar zygapophysial joint pain.' Spine 2000;25(10):1270-1277 (PMID recalled 10806505 — verify). Recalled design and results: 15 patients, selected by comparative/dual medial branch blocks requiring >=80% relief, treated with fluoroscopically guided lumbar medial branch neurotomy; at 12 months, 60% obtained at least 90% relief and 87% obtained at least 60% relief. I recall these two figures with reasonable specificity. The critical caveats to state alongside them: n=15 (very small), non-randomized, and performed with meticulous electrode placement by the technique's originators — so this is a best-case efficacy ceiling under ideal selection and technique, not an expected real-world rate. It is nonetheless the single most-cited number in the argument that selection rigor drives RFA success.
