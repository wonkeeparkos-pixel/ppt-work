### [보통] Composite indication set for diagnostic lumbar MBB, as it recurs across ASIPP / NASS coverage policy / Medicare LCDs — the five gates.

UNVERIFIED IN-SESSION; this is a synthesis of the recurring elements, not a quotation from any one document. (1) DURATION: chronic axial low back pain of at least 3 months, moderate-to-severe (LCDs commonly operationalize as >=3/10 or a stated functional limitation). (2) CONSERVATIVE CARE: documented failure of a trial of non-interventional management — the recalled range across policies is 6 weeks to 3 months, typically requiring more than one modality (e.g., physical therapy or a directed home exercise program PLUS pharmacotherapy such as NSAIDs/acetaminophen). NASS coverage policy is the one I recall as specifying ~6 weeks; several LCDs use 3 months. Report the range, not a single number. (3) PAIN CHARACTER: predominantly axial/paraspinal, NOT radicular — absence of clinical radiculopathy or neurogenic claudication, and no untreated neurological deficit. (4) NO UNADDRESSED RED FLAGS / no surgically remediable pathology: infection, fracture, tumor, cauda equina, progressive deficit must be excluded or already managed. (5) IMAGING: recent imaging required to exclude the above — explicitly NOT to demonstrate facet arthropathy, which is neither necessary nor sufficient. VERIFY the specific week/month thresholds against the current policy documents; they differ by payer and have been revised.
