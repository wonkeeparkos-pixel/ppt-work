### [미확인] Psychopathology measurably degrades MBB response — one of the few patient-selection variables with prospective supporting data.

UNVERIFIED IN-SESSION. Recalled source: Wasan AD, Jamison RN, Pham L, Tipirneni N, Nedeljkovic SS, Katz JN. 'Psychopathology predicts the outcome of medial branch blocks with corticosteroid for chronic axial low back or cervical pain: a prospective cohort study.' BMC Musculoskelet Disord 2009;10:22 (PMID recalled 19220916 — verify). Recalled finding: patients with high levels of depression/anxiety derived substantially less benefit from MBB with corticosteroid than those with low psychiatric symptom burden, with the effect large enough to matter clinically. I do NOT reliably recall the effect sizes or n — do not quote numbers. Selection implication for the deck: screening for depression/anxiety/catastrophizing before committing a patient to a diagnostic-then-RFA pathway is defensible on evidence, not just on temperament. Caution against over-reading: this is a modifier of therapeutic response, and its effect on the DIAGNOSTIC validity of an anesthetic block is a separate and less well-established question.
