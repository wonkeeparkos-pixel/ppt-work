## 이 문헌에서 인용된 근거 (1건)

### [확실] In practice clinicians under-angulate caudally: real-world fluoroscopic needle angles are 12-19 degrees shallower than the cadaver-derived optimum.

18 formalin-embalmed lumbosacral specimens 3D-modelled with virtual needles placed parallel to L1-L5 medial branches, compared with lateral fluoroscopic images from 200 patients who underwent lumbar medial branch denervation (L3-L5). Lowest mean caudal angle was at L1 (41.57 +/- 8.56 degrees; range 27.14-53.96). Cadaver-derived means exceeded patient-derived means by 18.94 degrees (L3), 15.06 degrees (L4) and 11.70 degrees (L5). Conclusion: optimisation requires GREATER caudal angulation than is typically used.
