### [보통] S1 contribution to the L5-S1 joint is claimed but remains controversial and is not part of the standard block prescription.

'Some authors have claimed that a branch from the S1 nerve root can run cephalad to supply a portion of the L5-S1 facet joint, although this point remains controversial.' The standard, evidence-based prescription for the L5-S1 joint remains the L4 medial branch plus the L5 dorsal ramus. Animal work shows the L5-S1 facet is multisegmentally innervated (rat: L1 to S1 DRG), which is sometimes invoked to explain residual pain after 'complete' blocks, but this is not directly transferable to human interventional practice.
