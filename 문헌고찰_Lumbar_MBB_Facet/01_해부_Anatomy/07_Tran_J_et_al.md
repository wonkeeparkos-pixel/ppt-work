## 이 문헌에서 인용된 근거 (4건)

### [확실] Branching pattern of the lumbar dorsal ramus at the lateral neck of the SAP is variable: 2 branches in 51.1%, 3 branches in 45.6%, 4 branches in 3.3%.

18 formalin-embalmed specimens, 90 lumbar dorsal rami, dissected/digitized/3D-modelled. 46 rami (51.1%) divided into 2 branches, 41 (45.6%) into 3, 3 (3.3%) into 4. Location of branching also varied: at the ANTERIOR quarter of the lateral neck of the SAP, 41 rami (45.6%) were still unbranched, whereas at the MIDPOINT of the lateral neck only 3 (3.3%) had not yet ramified. Practical implication: a lesion placed at the anterior/ventral end of the SAP neck more often captures a single undivided trunk.

*원 출처 표기*: `Tran J et al. High-fidelity 3D modelling of the lumbar dorsal rami. Interv Pain Med. 2024. (ScienceDirect pii S2772594424000219).`

### [확실] Because the L5 medial branch proper is short and variable, the clinical target at L5 is the L5 DORSAL RAMUS at a single point: the junction of the sacral ala and the root of the S1 superior articular process.

'A single target is identified at the junction of the sacral ala and S1 superior articular process, corresponding to the location of the L5 dorsal ramus.' The nerve is blocked in the groove between the ala of the sacrum and the S1 SAP. The L5 medial branch itself is described as variable, so the trunk is targeted instead. Notably the L5 level also has the LONGEST periosteal contact along the lateral neck of the SAP (81.54 ± 10.48%), favouring capture by a parallel electrode at this level.

*원 출처 표기*: `Dreyfuss P, Schwarzer AC, Lau P, Bogduk N. Spine. 1997;22(8):895-902 (PMID 9127924) for L5 dorsal ramus block technique; Tran J et al. Interv Pain Med. 2024;3(2):100414 for contact percentages.`

### [확실] Practical teaching summary of WHY the block is reliable: the medial branch is fixed against bone for a short, constant, level-dependent segment — and everything distal to that is variable.

Reliability derives from three anatomical facts: (1) the nerve is pinned in the SAP-TP groove and roofed by the MAL (Bogduk 1981); (2) the dorsal ramus bifurcates only ~3.4-3.6 mm from the superior border of the TP root at L1-L4 (1.9 mm at L5), so the target segment is short (Shuang 2015); (3) the length of periosteal contact along the lateral neck of the SAP varies from ~57% (L1) to ~82% (L5) of that neck, and the ramus has already branched into 2-4 branches by the midpoint of the SAP neck in 96.7% of specimens (Tran 2024). Corollaries: aim ventral/anterior along the SAP neck to catch an undivided trunk; lie parallel; use small volumes; expect ~11% physiologic false negatives.

*원 출처 표기*: `Synthesis of Bogduk N Spine 1981;6(2):162-167 (PMID 6456553); Shuang F et al. Medicine (Baltimore) 2015;94(52) PMC5291620; Tran J et al. Interv Pain Med 2024;3(2):100414; Tran J et al. Interv Pain Med 2024 (pii S2772594424000219); Kaplan M et al. Spine 1998;23(17):1847-1852.`
