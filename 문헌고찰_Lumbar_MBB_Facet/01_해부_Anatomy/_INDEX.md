## 확인하지 못한 항목 (gaps)

- WebFetch was blocked by this session's egress policy for every journal host (pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov, pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov, europepmc.org, sciencedirect.com, journals.lww.com, academic.oup.com, thejns.org, ajnr.org, wikimsk.org, musculoskeletalkey.com, viamedica.pl). All figures above were extracted from indexed abstract/summary text via WebSearch, NOT from full text. Numbers should be spot-verified against the primary PDFs before publication in a teaching deck.
- Exact page numbers/DOIs could not be confirmed for a few classic citations (Maigne 1991 Surg Radiol Anat page range; Macintosh/Valencia/Bogduk 1986 Clin Biomech page range; Tran 2022 Reg Anesth Pain Med volume/pages). Cited without fabricating those fields.
- The T12 medial branch contribution to the L1-2 joint is stated in several sources but I could not verify it against a primary lumbar dissection paper; most clinical texts label that joint using the bony-target convention ('L1 and L2'), which conflates nerve name with needle level. Treat the T12 attribution as probable but unverified.
- No quantitative human cadaveric prevalence figure was found for TRUE variant medial branch COURSE (e.g., a nerve running outside the SAP-TP groove) as distinct from variant BRANCHING pattern. The 51.1/45.6/3.3% figures describe branching, not aberrant course.
- No modern human cadaveric study quantifying an S1 dorsal ramus contribution to the L5-S1 joint was located; the claim remains explicitly controversial in the review literature.
- Nociceptor/mechanoreceptor DENSITY per unit area or per capsule in the human LUMBAR facet capsule was not found; the available data are presence/absence counts (Ashton n=14) and per-specimen proportions (McLain cervical 80%). The 24-unit electrophysiology data (Yamashita 1990) are rabbit, not human.
- Facet capsular capacity figures (1-2 mL vs 1-1.5 mL vs 1.5-2 cc) come from review-level and StatPearls sources; the original volumetric measurement study was not identified. Sources disagree modestly.
- One search summary reported a paravascular myelinated nerve fibre diameter of '0.2 micron' in synovial folds (Giles & Taylor 1987). This is physically implausible for a myelinated fibre and is almost certainly a transcription artifact; I have deliberately excluded it and reported only the corroborated 0.6-12 micrometre range.
- The '27 patients, 12.7 months, non-significant fat-subtracted multifidus CSA decrease' datapoint appeared in a review summary without a resolvable primary citation; treat as low-confidence until traced.
- Exact per-level needle angle tables from Tran 2024 (posterior half of SAP target, Pain Med 25(1):13-19) were not retrievable — only the study design and the comparison statement about 29-47 degree oblique approaches being impractical.
