## 이 문헌에서 인용된 근거 (3건)

### [확실] The lumbar dorsal ramus arises from the spinal nerve just outside the intervertebral foramen and, at L1-L4, divides into medial, intermediate and lateral branches supplying multifidus, longissimus and iliocostalis respectively.

Foundational dissection study of the human lumbar dorsal rami. L1-L4 dorsal rami tend to form three branches (medial, lateral, intermediate) distributed to multifidus, iliocostalis and longissimus. The division occurs at/near the upper margin of the transverse process, within or deep to the intertransverse ligament. Later 3D work (Tran et al., Reg Anesth Pain Med 2022) refined this: the L4 dorsal ramus regularly forms three branches, whereas at L1-L3 the lateral and intermediate branches may alternatively arise from a short common stem.

*원 출처 표기*: `Bogduk N, Wilson AS, Tynan W. The human lumbar dorsal rami. J Anat. 1982;134(Pt 2):383-397. PMID 7076562. Supplemented by Tran J et al., Reg Anesth Pain Med, 2022.`

### [확실] At L1-L4 the medial branch runs across the root/neck of the superior articular process (SAP) of the vertebra ONE LEVEL BELOW its parent nerve, in the groove at the SAP-transverse process (TP) junction.

The medial branch exits the intervertebral foramen, courses around the lateral neck of the SAP to reach the posterior compartment of the back, hugging the periosteum in the groove between the root of the SAP and the superior/upper border of the root of the TP. This bone-hugging segment is the fluoroscopic target. Example: the L3 medial branch crosses the L4 TP at the base of the L4 SAP.

*원 출처 표기*: `Bogduk N, Long DM. J Neurosurg. 1979;51(2):172-177. PMID 156249; Bogduk N, Wilson AS, Tynan W. J Anat. 1982;134:383-397. PMID 7076562.`

### [확실] The L5 dorsal ramus is anatomically unique: it is much LONGER than L1-L4, has NO lateral branch, and it is the PARENT TRUNK (not a medial branch) that lies on bone at the target site.

The L5 dorsal ramus arises just outside the L5-S1 foramen and runs dorsally at the bottom of a groove bounded medially by the S1 superior articular process and caudally/laterally by the ala of the sacrum. It gives off its medial branch only when it reaches the CAUDAL edge of the S1 SAP / caudal aspect of the L5-S1 joint; the medial branch then wraps medially around the posterior aspect of the L5-S1 joint and terminates in multifidus. The L5 dorsal ramus forms only a medial and an intermediate branch — it lacks a lateral branch because iliocostalis has no attachment at L5 (replaced by the iliolumbar ligament). Consistent with this, the dorsal-ramus bifurcation distance at L5 (1.87 ± 0.88 mm from the superior border of the TP root) is roughly half that at L1-L4.

*원 출처 표기*: `Bogduk N, Wilson AS, Tynan W. J Anat. 1982;134:383-397 (PMID 7076562); Tran J et al., Reg Anesth Pain Med 2022; Shuang F et al., Medicine (Baltimore) 2015;94(52), PMC5291620.`
