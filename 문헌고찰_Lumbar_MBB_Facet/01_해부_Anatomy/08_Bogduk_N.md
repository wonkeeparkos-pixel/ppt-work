## 이 문헌에서 인용된 근거 (5건)

### [확실] NAMING CONVENTION — the single most confusing point: the medial branch is named for its PARENT SEGMENTAL NERVE, but it crosses the transverse process of the vertebra ONE LEVEL BELOW. The procedural/fluoroscopic literature instead names the target by the vertebra the needle sits on.

Anatomical convention (Bogduk): 'L3 medial branch' = branch of the L3 dorsal ramus, and it runs over the L4 TP/SAP junction. Procedural convention: a needle resting on the L4 TP is commonly documented as an 'L4 MBB target' even though the nerve anesthetized is the L3 medial branch. Explicit statement in the procedural literature: 'The medial branch nerves run over the junction of the TP and SAP at one level below where it originates. To block the medial branch from L3 you target the TP and SAP at L4… The images use numbering based on the vertebral body being treated.' This dual usage is a documented source of documentation/coding error. Teaching rule: SPEAK IN NERVES, POINT IN BONES.

*원 출처 표기*: `Bogduk N. Spine. 1983;8(3):286-293 (PMID 6226119) for the anatomical convention; procedural convention documented in The Procedure Guide, Fluoroscopic Guided Lumbar Medial Branch Block; also flagged in AAPC coding discussions and Becker's ASC audit commentary.`

### [확실] DUAL INNERVATION: every lumbar zygapophysial joint receives an ascending (proximal) branch from the medial branch of its own segmental number and a descending (distal) branch from the medial branch one level above.

Bogduk 1983: 'Each lumbar medial branch innervates two adjacent zygapophysial joints and ramifies in multifidus, supplying only those fascicles which arise from the spinous process with the same segmental number as the nerve.' Therefore the joint Ln/Ln+1 is supplied by the Ln medial branch (ascending branch, arising as the nerve passes caudal to that joint) plus the Ln-1 medial branch (descending branch). To anesthetize one joint you must block TWO nerves, at TWO bony targets, at consecutive levels.

*원 출처 표기*: `Bogduk N. The innervation of the lumbar spine. Spine. 1983;8(3):286-293. PMID 6226119.`

### [확실] EXACT MAPPING TABLE (nerve names / bony targets) for L1-2 through L5-S1.

L1-2 joint: T12 medial branch (target = L1 TP/SAP junction) + L1 medial branch (target = L2 TP/SAP junction). L2-3 joint: L1 MB (at L2 TP/SAP) + L2 MB (at L3 TP/SAP). L3-4 joint: L2 MB (at L3 TP/SAP) + L3 MB (at L4 TP/SAP). L4-5 joint: L3 MB (at L4 TP/SAP) + L4 MB (at L5 TP/SAP). L5-S1 joint: L4 MB (at L5 TP/SAP) + L5 DORSAL RAMUS (at the junction of the sacral ala and the root of the S1 SAP). Corroborating primary statements: 'the L3-4 joint is innervated by the second and third lumbar medial branches that cross the grooves between the L3 SAP/TP and the L4 SAP/TP respectively'; 'the L4-5 joint is supplied by the medial branches originating from the L3 and L4 nerves'; 'the L5-S1 facet joint is innervated by the medial branch of L4 and the dorsal ramus of L5'. NOTE: the T12 contribution to L1-2 is less consistently reported than the lower levels — several clinical sources label that joint 'L1 and L2' using the bony-target convention rather than nerve names.

*원 출처 표기*: `Bogduk N. Spine. 1983;8(3):286-293 (PMID 6226119); Bogduk N, Long DM. J Neurosurg. 1979;51(2):172-177 (PMID 156249). T12 contribution to L1-2 also observed in dissection reports of thoracolumbar articular branches (Interv Pain Med 2024, pii S2772594424000190).`

### [확실] Non-articular targets of the lumbar medial branch: multifidus, interspinales, intertransversarii mediales, the interspinous ligament, and the periosteum/soft tissues from midline to the joint line.

Bogduk 1983: 'Medial branches supply the multifidus, intertransversarii mediales, interspinales, interspinous ligament, and the lumbar zygapophysial joints.' The medial branch weaves medially between multifidus fascicles to reach the interspinous space; it supplies the tissues from the midline out to the zygapophysial joint line. Interspinous ligament innervation is derived from the medial branches with nerve supply distributed evenly along the ligament, symmetric left/right and denser peripherally.

*원 출처 표기*: `Bogduk N. The innervation of the lumbar spine. Spine. 1983;8(3):286-293. PMID 6226119.`

### [확실] Multifidus is innervated UNISEGMENTALLY: each medial branch supplies only those multifidus fascicles arising from the spinous process bearing the SAME segmental number as the nerve.

Dissection showed the lumbar multifidus fibres are divided by distinct cleavage planes into five bands; each band arises from a single lumbar spinous process and is innervated unisegmentally. The muscle therefore comprises five myotomes such that fibres moving a given segment are innervated by the nerve of that segment. At each level fibres are arranged in superficial, intermediate and deep layers, except L5/S1 where no intermediate fascicles are found. Consequence: RFA of one medial branch denervates one segmental band of multifidus, and denervation of the band is unavoidable collateral of any effective lesion, because the nerve is one trunk.

*원 출처 표기*: `Macintosh JE, Valencia F, Bogduk N, Munro RR. The morphology of the human lumbar multifidus. Clin Biomech. 1986;1(4):196-204; segmental innervation reiterated in Bogduk N, Spine 1983;8(3):286-293 (PMID 6226119).`
