### [확실] Lumbar facet joint capsule: fibrous capsule ~1 mm thick, attaching approximately 2 mm peripheral to the articular margins, with an anteriorly situated SUPERIOR recess and a posteriorly directed INFERIOR recess.

Paired synovial joints with hyaline-cartilage articular surfaces bounded by a fibrous capsule of approximately 1 mm thickness. The capsule attaches to the superior and inferior articular processes about 2 mm peripheral to the articular margins. The synovial space has an anteriorly situated superior recess and a posteriorly directed inferior recess.
