## 이 문헌에서 인용된 근거 (1건)

### [보통] Contemporary RF hardware standard: 16-22 G cannula (18 G or larger preferred), >=10 mm active tip, 80-85 degrees C for 90-119 s, placed parallel to the nerve.

SIS-aligned practice: a 16- to 22-gauge cannula navigated under fluoroscopy with the tip parallel to the nerve; 'with conventional RFN, cannulae with a 10 mm active tip or larger is optimal' and '18-gauge or larger cannulas are advisable to increase the nerve capture rate and lesion size.' A majority of SIS survey respondents perform lumbar RFN by parallel approach with 18 G or larger and a 10 mm active tip at >=80-85 degrees C for 90-119 s. Tip gauge, tip length, temperature and time all substantially affect lesion size.
