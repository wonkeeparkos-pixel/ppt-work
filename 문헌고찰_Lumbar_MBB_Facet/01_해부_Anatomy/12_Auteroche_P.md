## 이 문헌에서 인용된 근거 (1건)

### [보통] MAJOR DISSENTING ANATOMY: Auteroche's dissection of 200 lumbar zygapophysial joints reported profound interindividual variability, with each joint receiving nerves from at least THREE spinal levels and from anterior, lateral and posterior aspects.

'The nerves supplying these joints are of multiple origins; the zygapophyseal articulations receive their innervation by the anterior, lateral or posterior aspect, and rather than following a strictly metameric pattern, such nerve supply involves at least three spinal levels.' Innervation varied within the same individual by level and by side. This directly contradicts Bogduk's clean two-level segmental model and is the classic counter-citation to the dual-innervation dogma. Report BOTH positions when teaching.
