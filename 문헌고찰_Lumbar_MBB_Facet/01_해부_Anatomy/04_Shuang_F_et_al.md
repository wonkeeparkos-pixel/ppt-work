## 이 문헌에서 인용된 근거 (3건)

### [확실] The fibro-osseous canal containing the medial branch is bounded by the accessory process, the mamillary process and the MAL; the canal near the accessory process is the recommended first-choice target.

Dissection of 12 adult cadaveric lumbar spines (24 sides). Distance from the dorsal ramus bifurcation to the superior border of the root of the transverse process: L1 3.52 ± 1.15 mm; L2 3.63 ± 1.36 mm; L3 3.46 ± 1.31 mm; L4 3.38 ± 1.24 mm; L5 1.87 ± 0.88 mm. The L5 value is significantly shorter than L1-L4; no significant difference among L1-L4. Authors conclude the fibro-osseous canal near the accessory process is the first-choice target for block/neurotomy.

*원 출처 표기*: `Shuang F et al. Clinical Anatomy and Measurement of the Medial Branch of the Spinal Dorsal Ramus. Medicine (Baltimore). 2015;94(52). PMC5291620.`

### [확실] The L5 dorsal ramus is anatomically unique: it is much LONGER than L1-L4, has NO lateral branch, and it is the PARENT TRUNK (not a medial branch) that lies on bone at the target site.

The L5 dorsal ramus arises just outside the L5-S1 foramen and runs dorsally at the bottom of a groove bounded medially by the S1 superior articular process and caudally/laterally by the ala of the sacrum. It gives off its medial branch only when it reaches the CAUDAL edge of the S1 SAP / caudal aspect of the L5-S1 joint; the medial branch then wraps medially around the posterior aspect of the L5-S1 joint and terminates in multifidus. The L5 dorsal ramus forms only a medial and an intermediate branch — it lacks a lateral branch because iliocostalis has no attachment at L5 (replaced by the iliolumbar ligament). Consistent with this, the dorsal-ramus bifurcation distance at L5 (1.87 ± 0.88 mm from the superior border of the TP root) is roughly half that at L1-L4.

*원 출처 표기*: `Bogduk N, Wilson AS, Tynan W. J Anat. 1982;134:383-397 (PMID 7076562); Tran J et al., Reg Anesth Pain Med 2022; Shuang F et al., Medicine (Baltimore) 2015;94(52), PMC5291620.`

### [확실] Practical teaching summary of WHY the block is reliable: the medial branch is fixed against bone for a short, constant, level-dependent segment — and everything distal to that is variable.

Reliability derives from three anatomical facts: (1) the nerve is pinned in the SAP-TP groove and roofed by the MAL (Bogduk 1981); (2) the dorsal ramus bifurcates only ~3.4-3.6 mm from the superior border of the TP root at L1-L4 (1.9 mm at L5), so the target segment is short (Shuang 2015); (3) the length of periosteal contact along the lateral neck of the SAP varies from ~57% (L1) to ~82% (L5) of that neck, and the ramus has already branched into 2-4 branches by the midpoint of the SAP neck in 96.7% of specimens (Tran 2024). Corollaries: aim ventral/anterior along the SAP neck to catch an undivided trunk; lie parallel; use small volumes; expect ~11% physiologic false negatives.
