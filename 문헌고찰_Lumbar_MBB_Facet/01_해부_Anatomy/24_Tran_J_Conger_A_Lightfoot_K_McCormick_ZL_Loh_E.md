## 이 문헌에서 인용된 근거 (2건)

### [확실] Even with modern optimised targeting, simulated lesions completely capture the medial branch in only ~42% of placements.

Twelve cannulae placed under fluoroscopic guidance targeting the L3, L4 and L5 medial branches in 2 embalmed cadaveric specimens, with dissection, digitisation, 3D modelling and lesion simulation. Complete capture (lesion overlapping the medial branch trunk or all of its distal branches) in 5/12 (41.7%); partial capture (at least one distal branch outside the lesion) in 4/12 (33.3%); no capture in 3/12 (25.0%). This is the strongest quantitative evidence that anatomic variability, not just technique, limits RFA success.

### [확실] Emerging two-lesion strategy: posterior portion of the lateral neck of the SAP PLUS the superior aspect of the sub-mammillary fossa, with defined fluoroscopic landmarks.

Refined fluoroscopic landmarks for the sub-mammillary fossa cannula: AP view — inferior and medial to the lateral boundary of the mamillary process; oblique view — inferior and medial to the mamillo-accessory notch and lateral to the inferior boundary of the facet joint line; lateral view — immediately inferior to the mamillary process. Rationale is to extend the length of nerve lesioned and more reliably capture the medial branch, and to permit a more practical parasagittal trajectory than the classical 29-47 degree oblique.
