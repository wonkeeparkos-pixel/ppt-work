## 이 문헌에서 인용된 근거 (1건)

### [확실] Injectate volume determines block specificity: 0.25 mL stays deep and on target; 0.5 mL consistently spreads dorsally to superficial muscle and to distal dorsal ramus branches.

Six cadavers with non-dissected lumbar spines; fluoroscopically guided posterior-oblique medial branch injections of 0.25 mL (one side) vs 0.50 mL (other side) of 9:1 Omnipaque 240 / 1% methylene blue. Both volumes adequately coated the medial branches. With 0.5 mL there was consistent dorsal spread to superficial muscles and to distal segments of the dorsal rami remote from the target; with 0.25 mL spread was contained within the deep and intermediate muscular layers close to the target. Authors concluded 0.5 mL may produce an adjacent-level block and reduce diagnostic specificity.
