## 이 문헌에서 인용된 근거 (1건)

### [보통] Consensus/guideline volume for a diagnostic lumbar MBB is <=0.5 mL per level (many practices use 0.25-0.3 mL).

'Injection of volume of local anesthetics should not exceed 0.5 mL for medial branch blocks'; audits of SIS-concordant practice report 'no more than 0.5 cc per unilateral level'. This is a direct consequence of the volume-spread data above and of the small capacity of the target compartment.
