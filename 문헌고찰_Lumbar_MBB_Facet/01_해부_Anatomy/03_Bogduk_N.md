## 이 문헌에서 인용된 근거 (4건)

### [확실] The mamillo-accessory ligament (MAL) converts the SAP-TP groove into an osseofibrous tunnel that FIXES the medial branch against bone — the anatomical justification for a reliable percutaneous block.

Gross dissection in 6 cadavers. The MAL bridges the mamillary process (on the SAP) and the accessory process (on the TP) of the SAME lumbar vertebra — it is therefore not a true ligament (it connects two points on one bone; structurally more tendon-like, interpreted as a remnant of transversospinal/semispinalis elements). It encloses the medial branch in an osseofibrous tunnel, 'which maintains the proximal course of the medial branch in a constant relationship to bone. This constancy allows for accurate percutaneous techniques to stimulate, anesthetize or destroy the medial branch.' Bogduk also reported the MAL is ossified in over 10% of lower lumbar vertebrae, which may interfere with some percutaneous denervation techniques, and may be a site of medial branch entrapment causing low back pain.

*원 출처 표기*: `Bogduk N. The lumbar mamillo-accessory ligament: its anatomical and neurosurgical significance. Spine. 1981;6(2):162-167. PMID 6456553.`

### [확실] Ossification of the MAL / mamillo-accessory foramen: reported prevalence spans roughly 10% to 73% depending on level, side, sex, age and method — sources genuinely disagree.

Bogduk 1981 (cadaver, gross dissection): ossified in 'over 10% of lower lumbar vertebrae'. Maigne et al. (203 dried lumbosacral spines): the mamillo-accessory FORAMEN is present at L5 in 26% on the left and 13.5% on the right, much less frequently at L4, and almost never above L4; authors proposed the foramina are a manifestation of osteoarthritic change that could irritate or compress the dorsal ramus. A 2024 osteological series (935 dried lumbar vertebrae from 187 donors, Khon Kaen University, Thailand) reported ossified MAL in 72.73% overall, highest at L5 on the left in females (76.92%); over 50% of ossified MALs occurred in donors aged 30-45 and frequency increased with age. A review places the literature range at approximately 10-72% depending on level and classification (partial notch vs complete foramen).

*원 출처 표기*: `Bogduk N. Spine. 1981;6(2):162-167 (PMID 6456553); Maigne JY, Maigne R, Guerin-Surville H. The lumbar mamillo-accessory foramen: a study of 203 lumbosacral spines. Surg Radiol Anat. 1991 (PMID 1905063); Surg Radiol Anat. 2024, doi:10.1007/s00276-024-03412-y; review PMID 31338226 / PMC6624327.`

### [확실] Ossified MAL is a proposed entrapment neuropathy of the medial branch and a proposed cause of technical failure of RFA.

'Ossification of the mamillo-accessory ligament is posited to result in nerve entrapment of the medial branch of the dorsal ramus causing zygapophyseal joint related low back pain,' and ossification 'may interfere with some percutaneous denervation techniques.' Practical teaching point: an ossified MAL creates a bony roof over the nerve that can deflect a cannula off the intended periosteal plane.

*원 출처 표기*: `Bogduk N. Spine. 1981;6(2):162-167 (PMID 6456553); review of MAL ossification PMID 31338226; The Hidden Anatomy of Low Back Pain: Uncovering the Impact of Mamillo-Accessory Ligament Ossification, J Funct Morphol Kinesiol, doi:10.3390/jfmk11010100 (PMC13028404).`

### [확실] Practical teaching summary of WHY the block is reliable: the medial branch is fixed against bone for a short, constant, level-dependent segment — and everything distal to that is variable.

Reliability derives from three anatomical facts: (1) the nerve is pinned in the SAP-TP groove and roofed by the MAL (Bogduk 1981); (2) the dorsal ramus bifurcates only ~3.4-3.6 mm from the superior border of the TP root at L1-L4 (1.9 mm at L5), so the target segment is short (Shuang 2015); (3) the length of periosteal contact along the lateral neck of the SAP varies from ~57% (L1) to ~82% (L5) of that neck, and the ramus has already branched into 2-4 branches by the midpoint of the SAP neck in 96.7% of specimens (Tran 2024). Corollaries: aim ventral/anterior along the SAP neck to catch an undivided trunk; lie parallel; use small volumes; expect ~11% physiologic false negatives.

*원 출처 표기*: `Synthesis of Bogduk N Spine 1981;6(2):162-167 (PMID 6456553); Shuang F et al. Medicine (Baltimore) 2015;94(52) PMC5291620; Tran J et al. Interv Pain Med 2024;3(2):100414; Tran J et al. Interv Pain Med 2024 (pii S2772594424000219); Kaplan M et al. Spine 1998;23(17):1847-1852.`
