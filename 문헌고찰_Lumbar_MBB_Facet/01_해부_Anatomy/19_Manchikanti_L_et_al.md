## 이 문헌에서 인용된 근거 (1건)

### [확실] Diagnostic standard built on this anatomy: DUAL comparative medial branch blocks with an >=80% relief threshold; reported prevalence of lumbar facet joint pain 29-60% with false-positive rates 27-63%.

ASIPP 2020 comprehensive guidelines: 80% pain relief is the criterion standard to define a positive response; ten diagnostic-accuracy studies using controlled comparative local anesthetic or placebo blocks with concordant relief >=80% gave prevalence 29-60% and false-positive rates 27-63%, with high variability. Level II evidence, moderate strength of recommendation.
