## 이 문헌에서 인용된 근거 (1건)

### [확실] Ossification of the MAL / mamillo-accessory foramen: reported prevalence spans roughly 10% to 73% depending on level, side, sex, age and method — sources genuinely disagree.

Bogduk 1981 (cadaver, gross dissection): ossified in 'over 10% of lower lumbar vertebrae'. Maigne et al. (203 dried lumbosacral spines): the mamillo-accessory FORAMEN is present at L5 in 26% on the left and 13.5% on the right, much less frequently at L4, and almost never above L4; authors proposed the foramina are a manifestation of osteoarthritic change that could irritate or compress the dorsal ramus. A 2024 osteological series (935 dried lumbar vertebrae from 187 donors, Khon Kaen University, Thailand) reported ossified MAL in 72.73% overall, highest at L5 on the left in females (76.92%); over 50% of ossified MALs occurred in donors aged 30-45 and frequency increased with age. A review places the literature range at approximately 10-72% depending on level and classification (partial notch vs complete foramen).
