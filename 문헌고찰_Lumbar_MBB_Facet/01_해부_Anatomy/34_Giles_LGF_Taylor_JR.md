## 이 문헌에서 인용된 근거 (1건)

### [확실] Human lumbar facet capsule and synovial folds contain small myelinated nerves 0.6-12 micrometres in diameter, 1-5 fibres per nerve, coursing INDEPENDENT of blood vessels — the morphological signature of nociceptive rather than vasomotor innervation.

High-power light microscopy plus transmission electron microscopy of human lower lumbar zygapophysial postero-medial fibrous capsules and their synovial folds. Small myelinated nerves 0.6-12 micrometres coursed through the synovial folds NOT in association with blood vessels, suggesting a nociceptive role. Companion paper: nerve diameter range 0.6-12 micrometres, with 1-5 fibres per nerve.
