## 이 문헌에서 인용된 근거 (1건)

### [보통] Capsular capacity is 1-2 mL (commonly quoted 1-1.5 mL); exceeding this ruptures the capsule and decompresses into the epidural space.

'The facet joint holds only a limited volume, 1-2 mL'; other sources state the lumbar facet accommodates 1 to 1.5 mL, and that injected volume should be 1-1.5 mL because larger volumes may rupture the joint capsule. Historically important: 'it was later realized that the capacity of the joint was less than the volume of injectate used in earlier studies, and decompression into the epidural space might have caused many false-positive responses' — a key reason intra-articular injection was displaced by medial branch blocks as the diagnostic standard. Sources vary slightly (1-1.5 vs 1.5-2 mL); the safe teaching figure is ~1-1.5 mL.
