### [확실] Electrophysiology: mechanosensitive afferents in the facet joint region are predominantly group III (thin myelinated), and ~29% are high-threshold (nociceptive).

Rabbit lumbar facet joint, laminectomy approach, single-unit recording. Twenty-four units identified in the facet joint region: 10 in the joint capsule, 12 at capsule-muscle/tendon border regions, 2 in ligamentum flavum. Conduction velocity: 2 units group IV (<2.5 m/s), 15 units group III (2.5-20 m/s), 7 units >20 m/s. Von Frey threshold: 7 units >6.0 g (high threshold), 13 units <6.0 g, 4 not examined. Seven units responded to movement of the joint. Species caveat: rabbit, not human.
