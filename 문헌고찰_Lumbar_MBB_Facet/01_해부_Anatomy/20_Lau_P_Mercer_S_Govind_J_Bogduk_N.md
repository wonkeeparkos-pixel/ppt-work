## 이 문헌에서 인용된 근거 (1건)

### [확실] Electrode must lie PARALLEL to the nerve: a perpendicular electrode may miss or only partially coagulate the medial branch.

Demonstration cadaver study with electrodes placed on and parallel to the L4 medial branch and the L5 dorsal ramus. 'If electrodes are placed perpendicular to the nerve, the nerve may escape coagulation, or be only partially coagulated.' Lau et al. recommended approximately 20 degrees of angulation from the sagittal plane to achieve parallel placement along the medial branch at the middle two-quarters of the lateral neck of the SAP.
