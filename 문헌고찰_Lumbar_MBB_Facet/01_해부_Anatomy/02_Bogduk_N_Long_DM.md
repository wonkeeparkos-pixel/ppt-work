## 이 문헌에서 인용된 근거 (4건)

### [확실] At L1-L4 the medial branch runs across the root/neck of the superior articular process (SAP) of the vertebra ONE LEVEL BELOW its parent nerve, in the groove at the SAP-transverse process (TP) junction.

The medial branch exits the intervertebral foramen, courses around the lateral neck of the SAP to reach the posterior compartment of the back, hugging the periosteum in the groove between the root of the SAP and the superior/upper border of the root of the TP. This bone-hugging segment is the fluoroscopic target. Example: the L3 medial branch crosses the L4 TP at the base of the L4 SAP.

*원 출처 표기*: `Bogduk N, Long DM. J Neurosurg. 1979;51(2):172-177. PMID 156249; Bogduk N, Wilson AS, Tynan W. J Anat. 1982;134:383-397. PMID 7076562.`

### [확실] Bogduk & Long (1979) disproved the existence of discrete 'articular nerves' and re-defined the correct denervation target — this is the paper that invalidated the original Shealy-era facet denervation targets.

Dissections of L1-L5 dorsal rami in human cadavers showed there is no separate 'posterior primary ramus articular nerve'; instead, at L1-L4 the dorsal rami divide into medial and lateral branches within/deep to the intertransverse ligaments, each medial branch running across the root of the adjacent SAP and turning medially beneath the mamillo-accessory ligament. Proximal (ascending) branches innervate the adjacent zygapophysial joint; distal (descending) branches innervate the next lower joint.

*원 출처 표기*: `Bogduk N, Long DM. The anatomy of the so-called 'articular nerves' and their relationship to facet denervation in the treatment of low-back pain. J Neurosurg. 1979;51(2):172-177. PMID 156249.`

### [확실] EXACT MAPPING TABLE (nerve names / bony targets) for L1-2 through L5-S1.

L1-2 joint: T12 medial branch (target = L1 TP/SAP junction) + L1 medial branch (target = L2 TP/SAP junction). L2-3 joint: L1 MB (at L2 TP/SAP) + L2 MB (at L3 TP/SAP). L3-4 joint: L2 MB (at L3 TP/SAP) + L3 MB (at L4 TP/SAP). L4-5 joint: L3 MB (at L4 TP/SAP) + L4 MB (at L5 TP/SAP). L5-S1 joint: L4 MB (at L5 TP/SAP) + L5 DORSAL RAMUS (at the junction of the sacral ala and the root of the S1 SAP). Corroborating primary statements: 'the L3-4 joint is innervated by the second and third lumbar medial branches that cross the grooves between the L3 SAP/TP and the L4 SAP/TP respectively'; 'the L4-5 joint is supplied by the medial branches originating from the L3 and L4 nerves'; 'the L5-S1 facet joint is innervated by the medial branch of L4 and the dorsal ramus of L5'. NOTE: the T12 contribution to L1-2 is less consistently reported than the lower levels — several clinical sources label that joint 'L1 and L2' using the bony-target convention rather than nerve names.

*원 출처 표기*: `Bogduk N. Spine. 1983;8(3):286-293 (PMID 6226119); Bogduk N, Long DM. J Neurosurg. 1979;51(2):172-177 (PMID 156249). T12 contribution to L1-2 also observed in dissection reports of thoracolumbar articular branches (Interv Pain Med 2024, pii S2772594424000190).`

### [보통] Morphology of the two articular branches: a short ASCENDING branch to the inferior capsule of the joint above the nerve's bony crossing, and a longer sinuous DESCENDING branch to the superior capsule of the joint below.

'Ascending branches arise from the medial branch as it passes caudal to the facet joint; these branches are short and ramify in the inferior aspect of the facet joint capsule. A descending articular branch arises from the medial branch as it crosses the superolateral corner of the transverse process and follows a sinuous course between the fascicles of multifidus to reach the superior aspect of the capsule of the facet joint below.' The descending branch's intramuscular course means it is captured by a lesion only proximally, at the bone.

*원 출처 표기*: `Described in the lumbar/thoracic articular-branch literature; the ascending/descending scheme is attributed to Chua & Bogduk and consistent with Bogduk & Long, J Neurosurg 1979;51(2):172-177 (PMID 156249).`
