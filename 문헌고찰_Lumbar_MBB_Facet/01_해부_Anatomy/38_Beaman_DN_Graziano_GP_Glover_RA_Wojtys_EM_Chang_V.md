## 이 문헌에서 인용된 근거 (1건)

### [확실] Substance P immunoreactivity is demonstrable in degenerated human lumbar facet joints harvested at surgery (n=16).

Sixteen adult human lumbar facet joints harvested from patients undergoing lumbar spine procedures (degenerative disc disease, adult spinal deformity, facet degenerative arthritis, degenerative spondylolisthesis). H&E plus monoclonal anti-substance P immunohistochemistry. All facets grossly showed degenerative change with cartilage surface irregularity and fibrillation.
