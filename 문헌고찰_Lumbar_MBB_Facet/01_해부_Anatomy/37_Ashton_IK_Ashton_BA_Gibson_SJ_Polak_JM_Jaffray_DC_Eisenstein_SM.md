## 이 문헌에서 인용된 근거 (1건)

### [확실] Quantified neuropeptide profile of the human lumbar facet capsule (n=14 capsules) — and the ligamentum flavum is essentially aneural by comparison.

Antisera to PGP 9.5 (general neuronal marker), CGRP and substance P (sensory), VIP and CPON/neuropeptide Y (autonomic). In the facet capsule (n=14): PGP 9.5-immunoreactive nerves in 12 specimens; CGRP-immunoreactive in 5; free fibres immunoreactive for substance P in 3; VIP in 5; CPON-immunoreactive perivascular nerves in 9. Nerves occurred both perivascularly and as free fibres in the stroma. The title finding is that these fibres are present in the facet capsule but NOT in ligamentum flavum.
