## 이 문헌에서 인용된 근거 (1건)

### [확실] Post-RFA multifidus change: EMG denervation is consistent, but MRI-detectable SEGMENTAL atrophy is not — the literature disagrees.

Dreyfuss et al. (prospective, 5 patients, unilateral successful lumbar medial branch RFN, MRI at 17-26 months, 3 blinded radiologists): diffuse lumbar multifidus atrophy was detectable on MRI but radiologists could NOT reliably identify which side or which segments had been lesioned; all patients showed multifidus denervation on EMG at 6 weeks with sustained relief at 12 months. Counter-evidence: a 2024 study of unilateral RFN measured functional cross-sectional area on pre- and >=2-year post-procedure MRI and found the RFN side had a greater increase in multifidus fatty atrophy than the contralateral side (PMID 38635483). A quantitative study of 27 patients found a decrease in fat-subtracted multifidus CSA at a mean of 12.7 months post-RFA that did not reach statistical significance. A separate study found statistically significant reduction in multifidus shear modulus vs age/sex-matched controls at a mean of 11.42 months (PMID 35832607). A 2025 narrative review concludes the evidence base is scarce and heterogeneous, most studies enrolling fewer than 30 subjects.
