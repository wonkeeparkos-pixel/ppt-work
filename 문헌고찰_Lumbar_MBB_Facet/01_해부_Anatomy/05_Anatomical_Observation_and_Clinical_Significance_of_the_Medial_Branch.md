### [확실] Quantitative geometry of the medial branch as it enters the fibro-osseous canal (L1-L4): entry angle ~65-68 degrees to the MAL; ~4.7-5.2 mm caudal to the mamillary process.

Anatomical study of 8 human specimens. Angle between the medial branch and the MAL at the fibro-osseous canal entrance: L1 65 ± 3 degrees, L2 66 ± 3 degrees, L3 68 ± 3 degrees, L4 67 ± 4 degrees. Distance from the caudal aspect of the mamillary process to the medial branch at the canal entrance: L1 4.66 ± 1.02 mm, L2 4.80 ± 1.08 mm, L3 5.15 ± 1.23 mm, L4 5.07 ± 0.99 mm. The medial branch originates close to the root of the posterolateral SAP of the vertebra below, and after passing the MAL turns medially and caudally into multifidus.
