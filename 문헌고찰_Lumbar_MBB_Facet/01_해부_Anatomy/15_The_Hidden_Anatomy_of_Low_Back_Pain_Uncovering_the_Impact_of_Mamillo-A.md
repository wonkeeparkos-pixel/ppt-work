## 이 문헌에서 인용된 근거 (1건)

### [확실] Ossified MAL is a proposed entrapment neuropathy of the medial branch and a proposed cause of technical failure of RFA.

'Ossification of the mamillo-accessory ligament is posited to result in nerve entrapment of the medial branch of the dorsal ramus causing zygapophyseal joint related low back pain,' and ossification 'may interfere with some percutaneous denervation techniques.' Practical teaching point: an ossified MAL creates a bony roof over the nerve that can deflect a cannula off the intended periosteal plane.
