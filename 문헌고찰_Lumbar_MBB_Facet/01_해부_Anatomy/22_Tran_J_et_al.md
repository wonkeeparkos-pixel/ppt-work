## 이 문헌에서 인용된 근거 (1건)

### [확실] Osteological quantification of the traditional (middle two-quarters of SAP neck) approach: 29.3-47.2 degrees lateral to parasagittal and 33.5-49.2 degrees caudal.

Ten lumbar vertebral columns; needles placed for parallel alignment along the medial branch at the middle two-quarters of the lateral neck of the SAP. Mean needle angles 29.29 +/- 17.82 degrees to 47.22 +/- 16.27 degrees lateral to the parasagittal plane (posterior view) and 33.53 +/- 10.23 degrees to 49.19 +/- 10.69 degrees caudal to the superior vertebral endplate (lateral view). A companion study using 12 disarticulated lumbosacral spines quantified angles for the more parasagittal target at the POSTERIOR half of the lateral neck of the SAP, noting that oblique approaches of 29-47 degrees are 'difficult and/or impractical' in vivo.
