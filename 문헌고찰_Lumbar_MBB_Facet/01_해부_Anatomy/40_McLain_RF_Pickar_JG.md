## 이 문헌에서 인용된 근거 (1건)

### [확실] Encapsulated mechanoreceptors are present in human thoracic and lumbar facet capsules but LESS consistently than in cervical capsules.

Whole facet capsules from seven healthy adult patients, gold chloride staining, receptor endings classified by Freeman & Wyke criteria (encapsulation, identifiable morphometry, consistent morphometry on serial sections). A consistent but small population of receptors had previously been found in cervical facets; innervation of thoracic and lumbar levels is less consistent, suggesting proprioceptive function is less refined in the thoracolumbar spine. In the cervical series, clearly identifiable mechanoreceptors were found in 80% of specimens, including Type I, Type II and Type III receptors plus small unencapsulated (free/nociceptive) endings, the latter located in sub-synovial, loose areolar and dense capsular tissue.
