## 이 문헌에서 인용된 근거 (1건)

### [확실] The proportion of the lateral neck of the SAP in direct periosteal contact with the medial branch increases caudally, from ~57% at L1 to ~82% at L5.

70 lumbar dorsal rami from 14 formalin-embalmed specimens, dissected, digitized and 3D-modelled. Mean percentage of the lateral neck of the SAP in contact with the medial branch ranged from 57.39 ± 10.72% (L1) to 81.54 ± 10.48% (L5). Clinically this means the length of nerve available for capture by a parallel electrode is level-dependent and shortest at upper lumbar levels.
