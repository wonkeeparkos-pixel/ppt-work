### [보통] Laplante/DePalma 2012 — referral patterns into the limb OVERLAP between disc, facet and SIJ, which is why leg/thigh/hip-girdle location cannot separate the three structures.

Objective: examine the individual and combined relationship of AGE, HIP/GIRDLE PAIN, LEG PAIN and THIGH PAIN with a source of IDD, facet joint pain or SIJ pain in consecutive chronic LBP patients. Stated background: 'discogenic, facet joint, and sacroiliac joint mediated axial low back pain may be associated with OVERLAPPING pain referral patterns into the lower limb, and differences between pain referral patterns for these three structures have not been systematically investigated.'
