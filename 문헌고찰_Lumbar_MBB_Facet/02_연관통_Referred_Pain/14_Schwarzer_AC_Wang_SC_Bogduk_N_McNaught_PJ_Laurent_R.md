## 이 문헌에서 인용된 근거 (1건)

### [확실] Schwarzer 1995 — prevalence 40% (95% CI 27–53%) by single IA blocks with saline control, and NO clinical feature discriminated facet-joint pain.

63 chronic LBP patients studied prospectively (57 completed); detailed history and physical exam, then IA zygapophysial joint injections of 0.5% bupivacaine starting at the symptomatic level, to a maximum of three levels or until pain abolished; normal-saline injections into paraspinal muscle served as controls. Result: 23/57 (40%; 95% CI 27–53%) failed to obtain relief from saline but obtained relief from one or more IA local anaesthetic injections. Verbatim: 'None of the historical features or clinical tests could discriminate those patients with and those without zygapophysial joint pain.' Note the 40% here is a SINGLE-block figure and is inflated relative to double-block estimates.
