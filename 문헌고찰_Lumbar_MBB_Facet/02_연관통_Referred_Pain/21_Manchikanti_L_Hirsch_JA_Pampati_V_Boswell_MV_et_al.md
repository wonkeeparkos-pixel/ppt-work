### [확실] Manchikanti 2020 — pooled prevalence of lumbar facet joint pain 34.1% (95% CI 28.8–39.8%) with false-positive rate 49.8% (95% CI 42.7–56.8%).

Assessment of prevalence and false-positive rates of diagnostic lumbar facet joint nerve blocks. Prevalence 34.1% (95% CI 28.8%, 39.8%); FALSE-POSITIVE RATE 49.8% (95% CI 42.7%, 56.8%). i.e. roughly HALF of single positive blocks are false positives — the quantitative justification for dual comparative blocks and, by extension, for not trusting any clinical or pain-map-based selection.
