## 이 문헌에서 인용된 근거 (1건)

### [확실] Laslett 2004 — Revel's criteria FAILED external validation: sensitivity <17% (vs Revel's 92%), specificity ~90–93%.

Prospective blinded concurrent reference-standard validity design; consecutive chronic LBP patients completed pain drawings and psychosocial/disability questionnaires, had a clinical examination, then lumbar zygapophysial joint blocks. Verbatim: 'Earlier results were not replicated' — Revel's criteria showed LOW sensitivity (<17%) and HIGH specificity (~90%). Absence of pain on cough/sneeze only just reached significance (p = 0.05) in one model. Authors' conclusion: 'Revel's criteria are UNSUITABLE as a clinical screening test to select chronic LBP patients for initial ZJ blocks', although they may identify a small subset (11% of patients) likely to respond to an initial block (specificity 93%). Sensitivity 92% → <17% between two studies of the same criteria is the most dramatic replication failure in this literature and is a perfect teaching slide.
