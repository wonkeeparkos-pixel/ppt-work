## 이 문헌에서 인용된 근거 (2건)

### [보통] McCall, Park & O'Brien 1979 — intracapsular AND pericapsular injections at L1-2 and L4-5 in normal volunteers; upper lumbar joints are MORE sensitive than lower, and upper/lower referral areas OVERLAP.

Departments of Radiology and Spinal Disorders, Robert Jones & Agnes Hunt Orthopaedic Hospital, Oswestry. Method: intracapsular and pericapsular injections restricted to the L1-2 and L4-5 levels in normal volunteer subjects. Verbatim conclusions: 'The areas of pain referral indicate overlap between the upper and lower lumbar spine' and 'the pericapsular and intrafacetal pain referral areas are similar and the upper lumbar spine is more sensitive than the lower.' Two teaching consequences: (1) referral overlap between the top and bottom of the lumbar spine was demonstrated in 1979, decades before Fukui — this is not a new observation; (2) PERI-capsular injectate produces the same map as INTRA-articular injectate, so a 'positive' provocation response does not prove the injectate was intra-articular. Commonly cited in reviews for upper-lumbar referral to flank/groin and lower-lumbar referral to buttock/trochanter/lateral thigh, but I could not verify those specific zone words from the primary abstract. CLASSIC (pre-1990).

*원 출처 표기*: `McCall IW, Park WM, O'Brien JP. 'Induced pain referral from posterior lumbar elements in normal subjects.' Spine 1979;4(5):441–446`

### [확실] Fukui's own conclusion is the money quote for 'maps cannot localise the level': distributions were SIMILAR across levels and overlap was CONSIDERABLE.

Verbatim from the abstract: 'The distribution of referred pain from the L1/2 to L5/S1 zygapophyseal joints, and the medial branches of the dorsal rami from L1 to L5 were similar for each level stimulated, and the overlap of referred pain between each level was considerable.' Combined with Marks 1989 ('no consistent segmental or sclerotomal pattern' in 385 observations) and McCall 1979 ('overlap between the upper and lower lumbar spine'), all three independent provocation datasets converge on the same negative conclusion. This is the strongest evidence chain in the whole topic and should carry the teaching slide.
