### [보통] Laslett 2006 — clinical prediction rules for SCREENING (single) blocks: absence of centralization is the key variable, plus a 3-of-5 cluster.

Predictors of a positive screening lumbar zygapophysial joint block: CENTRALIZATION ABSENT on repeated-movement (McKenzie) testing, PLUS three or more of five signs — age > 50, symptoms best when walking, symptoms best when sitting, paraspinal onset of pain, positive extension-rotation test. IMPORTANT CAVEAT for the deck: these rules predict a SCREENING (uncontrolled, single) block, which itself has a false-positive rate approaching 50% — so the rule predicts a test that is itself unreliable.
