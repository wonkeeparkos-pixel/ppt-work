## 이 문헌에서 인용된 근거 (1건)

### [보통] Han 2023 (eClinicalMedicine) — the most recent systematic review; 62 studies (14 on the facet joint) and informative tests DO exist, but no clinical referral-pattern test reached the threshold for facet joints in the data I could verify.

MEDLINE, CINAHL, EMBASE searched March 2006 – 25 Jan 2023. 62 studies included: 35 disc, 14 facet joint, 11 SIJ, 2 all three. QUADAS-2 risk of bias; informative thresholds pre-set as +LR ≥ 2 and −LR ≤ 0.5. Verified numeric results are for the DISC: MRI disc degeneration +LR 2.53 (95% CI 1.57–4.07), −LR 0.15 (0.09–0.24); annular fissure +LR 2.88 (2.02–4.10), −LR 0.24 (0.10–0.55). The review states informative tests were found 'for the disc, sacroiliac joint, AND the facet joint', but I could not verify which facet-joint index test met the threshold or its LR — do not attribute a specific facet LR without reading the paper.
