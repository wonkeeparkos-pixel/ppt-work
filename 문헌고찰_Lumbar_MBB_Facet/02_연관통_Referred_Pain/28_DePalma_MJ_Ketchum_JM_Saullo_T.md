## 이 문헌에서 인용된 근거 (1건)

### [확실] DePalma 2011 — source of chronic LBP by controlled diagnostic procedures: IDD 42%, facet joint 31%, SIJ 18%; facet pain probability RISES with age.

378 cases from 358 patients reviewed; 170 cases from 156 patients who underwent diagnostic procedures were included. Prevalence: internal disc disruption 42%, facet joint pain 31%, sacroiliac joint pain 18%. Patients with IDD were significantly YOUNGER. Increasing age was associated with DECREASED probability of IDD and INCREASED probability of facet joint pain and SIJ pain as the source, up to approximately age 70. This is the empirical basis for Revel's 'age > 65' criterion.
