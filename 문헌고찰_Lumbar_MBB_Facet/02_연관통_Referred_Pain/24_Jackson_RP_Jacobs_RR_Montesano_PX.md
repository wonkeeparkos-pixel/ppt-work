### [보통] Jackson 1988 (Volvo Award) — 390 patients, 127 variables, no clinical parameter reliably predicted response to facet blocks.

IA injections of 1 mL 0.5% bupivacaine + 2 mg (0.5 mL) triamcinolone. 127 variables evaluated in 390 patients. Greater relief was ASSOCIATED with: older age, history of low back pain, normal gait, maximum pain on extension after forward flexion in standing, ABSENCE OF LEG PAIN, muscle spasm, and aggravation of pain with Valsalva. Authors' conclusion: the facet joints were NOT commonly the single or primary cause of LBP in most patients, and they were unable to identify clinical parameters that predicted response. Note the contradiction with Revel: Jackson found ABSENCE OF LEG PAIN predictive, whereas the provocation literature (Mooney, Fukui, Marks) shows facet joints routinely refer into the leg. Present as a genuine unresolved tension. CLASSIC (1988).
