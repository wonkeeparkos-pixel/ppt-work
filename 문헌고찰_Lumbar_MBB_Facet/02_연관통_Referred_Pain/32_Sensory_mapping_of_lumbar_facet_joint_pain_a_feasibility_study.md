## 이 문헌에서 인용된 근거 (1건)

### [확실] Modern reframing: suprathreshold sensory stimulation at the RF cannula reproduces the patient's own pain field in 14/15 patients — mapping works PROSPECTIVELY per-patient even though population maps do not localise.

15 participants, all of whom had already had diagnostic blocks. After image-guided placement of RF cannulae, the sensory detection threshold was obtained with 50 Hz stimulation, then SUPRATHRESHOLD stimulation applied; participants mapped the stimulated area against their pre-procedure pain diagram. Results: ALL 15 reported pain or paraesthesia during suprathreshold stimulation; 14 of 15 reported COMPLETE coverage of their usual painful area by suprathreshold stimulation of the nerves scheduled for denervation. Conclusion: lumbar facet joint pain can be mapped by suprathreshold sensory stimulation, 'which has the potential to introduce objectivity during RF denervation.' TEACHING FRAME for the deck: abandon the population pain map as a level-selection tool; use INDIVIDUAL intraoperative sensory mapping instead. That is the practical resolution of the whole topic.
