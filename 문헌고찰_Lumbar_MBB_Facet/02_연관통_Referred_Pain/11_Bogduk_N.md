### [확실] WHY the maps overlap so heavily — mechanism 1: convergence on second-order neurons (somatic referred pain), not root compression.

Bogduk's formulation: somatic referred pain arises from noxious stimulation of nerve endings in spinal structures (disc, zygapophysial joint, SIJ) and is perceived in regions sharing the same segmental innervation; radicular pain by contrast requires mechanical stimulation of the dorsal root or DRG. 'The physiological basis of referred pain is convergence. Common neurons in the spinal cord and thalamus receive sensory input from different peripheral sites and relay this onto higher centers. Under those conditions, and in the absence of additional sensory input to clarify the situation, the brain is unable to identify the source of the pain accurately and attributes it erroneously to the ENTIRE AREA subtended by the common neurons.' Because the lumbar dorsal horn pools afferents from back AND lower limb across multiple segments, the perceived area is inherently large and blurred — a wide, overlapping map is the EXPECTED output of the mechanism, not an artefact of sloppy studies.
