## 이 문헌에서 인용된 근거 (1건)

### [확실] Van den Heuvel 2024 (WIP/Pain Practice evidence-based guideline) — current position: NO gold standard clinical diagnosis; a single diagnostic/prognostic MBB confirms; FN/FP rates 25–49%.

Clinical picture 'suggestive' of lumbar facet pain: UNILATERAL LOCALIZED back pain WITHOUT referral to the LOWER LEG, worsened by movement, with paravertebral tenderness on palpation; aching pain worsened by activity, typically WITHOUT neuropathic characteristics or a radicular distribution. Explicit statement that there is NO gold standard for diagnosing lumbar facet pain. Recommendation: a single diagnostic/prognostic medial branch block (IA block in selected cases); if ≥50% pain reduction, proceed to radiofrequency. False-negative and false-positive rates of a diagnostic/prognostic MBB vary 25–49%, influenced by age, comorbid spinal pathology, opioid use and psychosocial factors. Note this guideline accepts a SINGLE block (a deliberate divergence from the ASIPP dual-comparative-block/≥80% position) — a live guideline disagreement worth showing.
