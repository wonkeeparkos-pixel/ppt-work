## 이 문헌에서 인용된 근거 (1건)

### [미확인] Schwarzer 1994 (Pain) established the high false-positive rate of UNCONTROLLED single diagnostic blocks — the reason single blocks over-diagnose facet pain.

'The false-positive rate of uncontrolled diagnostic blocks of the lumbar zygapophysial joints.' Pain 1994;58(2):195–200, PMID 7816487. This paper is the origin of the requirement for comparative/placebo-controlled blocks. (I did not retrieve the exact percentage from an accessible source — the commonly quoted figure is ~38%, but I could not verify it and it should be checked against the primary text before being put on a slide.)
