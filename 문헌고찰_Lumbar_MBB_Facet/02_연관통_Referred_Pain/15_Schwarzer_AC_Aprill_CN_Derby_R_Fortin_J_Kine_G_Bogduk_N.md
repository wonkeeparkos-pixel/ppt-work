## 이 문헌에서 인용된 근거 (1건)

### [확실] Schwarzer 1994 'test of two models' — 176 patients; the Fairbank and the Helbig & Lee clinical criteria for facet pain BOTH failed against confirmatory blocks.

176 consecutive chronic LBP patients with no prior lumbar surgery. History + examination, then zygapophysial joint injections or medial branch blocks with LIGNOCAINE; responders received CONFIRMATORY blocks with BUPIVACAINE (i.e. comparative local anaesthetic design). Verbatim result: 'None of the clinical features tested was found to be associated with response to the confirmatory block.' Conclusion: the Fairbank et al. and the Helbig & Lee criteria 'were shown to be unreliable in distinguishing pain of zygapophysial joint origin from pain of other origins.' This is the death certificate for pre-1990 'facet syndrome' clinical checklists.
