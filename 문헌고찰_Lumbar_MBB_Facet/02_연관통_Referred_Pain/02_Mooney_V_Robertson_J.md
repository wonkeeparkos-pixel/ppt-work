## 이 문헌에서 인용된 근거 (1건)

### [확실] Mooney & Robertson 1976 — the founding provocation study; hypertonic saline into facet joints reproduced 'disc-syndrome'-like back + lower-limb pain in asymptomatic volunteers AND patients.

Fluoroscopically confirmed intra-articular lumbar facet injections. Injection of hypertonic (irritant) saline produced severe local pain plus radiating pain into the BUTTOCK and POSTERIOR THIGH in healthy individuals; in patients the referral patterns were described as 'indistinguishable from the pain complaints frequently associated with the disk syndrome.' They further reported that straight-leg-raise limitation and diminished reflexes could be ABOLISHED by precise intra-articular local anaesthetic — the origin of the 'facet syndrome' concept and of the diagnostic-therapeutic block. Builds on Hirsch 1963. CLASSIC (pre-1990).
