## 이 문헌에서 인용된 근거 (1건)

### [확실] DePalma 2011 (PM&R) — pain LOCATION does carry some information, but the discriminating feature is midline vs paramidline, NOT the distal referral zone.

Patients pointed to their single most painful location, recorded as MIDLINE (over the spinous processes) or PARAMIDLINE (>1 fingerbreadth lateral to midline). Result: in internal disc disruption, significantly GREATER percentages reported MIDLINE pain and significantly LOWER percentages reported paramidline pain, compared with facet joint pain or SIJ pain. Conclusion: presence of midline LBP increases the probability of lumbar IDD. TEACHING NUANCE: this is the one location-based finding that survived — and it is about the AXIAL location of maximal pain, not about which leg zone the pain refers to. It does not identify the segmental LEVEL, only the structure.
