## 이 문헌에서 인용된 근거 (1건)

### [보통] Revel's criteria (1998) — the ONE published positive clinical prediction model: 5 of 7 criteria gave sensitivity 92%, specificity 80%.

Prospective randomised comparison of IA facet injection of 1 mL 2% lidocaine vs 1 mL sodium chloride in 80 patients with LBP; prevalence 31% using a ≥75% pain-relief threshold. The seven items: (1) age > 65 years; (2) pain WELL RELIEVED by recumbency; and pain NOT exacerbated by (3) coughing, (4) forward flexion, (5) rising from flexion, (6) extension, (7) extension-rotation. Threshold: ≥5 of 7 present. Reported performance: sensitivity 92%, specificity 80%. NOTE the paradox that makes this a good teaching slide: the criteria are essentially 'pain that behaves like NOTHING mechanical' — the model selects for absence of provocation, not for a referral pattern. Referral pattern is NOT one of Revel's seven items.
