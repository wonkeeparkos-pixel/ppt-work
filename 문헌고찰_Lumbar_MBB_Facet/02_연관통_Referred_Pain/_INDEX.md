If the deck needs a number, the primary texts of Fukui 1997, Marks 1989 and Schwarzer 1995 must be read directly.

- McCall 1979 specific referral ZONE WORDS (flank, groin, iliac crest, trochanter, lateral thigh) come from secondary paraphrase, not from the primary abstract, which reported only 'overlap between upper and lower lumbar spine' and 'upper lumbar more sensitive than lower'.

- Laplante/DePalma 2012 odds ratios for hip-girdle / thigh / leg pain vs source were not retrievable.
- Proportion of block-CONFIRMED symptomatic joints by level (i.e. the lumbar equivalent of Cooper's cervical C2-3 36% / C5-6 35% / C6-7 17%) does not appear to exist in an accessible source. The L4-5 > L5-S1 predominance is asserted in reviews and supported by OA-imaging prevalence (45.1% / 38.2%) and by biomechanics, but a block-confirmed level distribution with percentages was not found. Do NOT present the OA-imaging percentages as symptomatic-level percentages.
- Revel 1998 sens 92% / spec 80% comes from secondary sources quoting the paper; the primary abstract text I could reach did not restate them. Confirm before use.
