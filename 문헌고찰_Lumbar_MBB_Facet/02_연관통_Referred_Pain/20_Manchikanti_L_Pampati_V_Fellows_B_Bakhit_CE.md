### [확실] Manchikanti 2000 — 'The inability of the clinical picture to characterize pain from facet joints': prevalence 42%, false-positive rate 37%, and history/clinical/radiological features of NO assistance.

Prevalence of lumbar facet-joint-mediated pain in chronic LBP established at 42% using CONTROLLED COMPARATIVE local anaesthetic diagnostic blocks, with a false-positive rate of 37%. Verbatim conclusion: facet joint mediated pain is common in chronic LBP non-responsive to conservative care, 'but the history, clinical features, and radiological features are of NO significance or assistance in making the diagnosis of facet joint mediated pain with certainty.'
