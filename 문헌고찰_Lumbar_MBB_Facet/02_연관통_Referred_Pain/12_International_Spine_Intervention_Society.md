### [확실] WHY the maps overlap — mechanism 2 (anatomical, and the one most useful clinically): every lumbar facet joint has DUAL segmental innervation.

Each lumbar zygapophysial joint is innervated by the medial branch AT its level and by the medial branch of the level ABOVE. Consequence for blocks: to anaesthetise the L4/5 joint you must block the L3 AND L4 medial branches. Consequence for referral maps: a single joint feeds nociception into TWO segmental levels of the dorsal horn simultaneously, so its referral field is by construction a union of two segmental fields — adjacent joints therefore necessarily share large parts of their maps. This is the anatomical reason a level-specific lumbar pain map is not achievable even in principle, and it is the cleanest thing to teach alongside the Fukui table.
