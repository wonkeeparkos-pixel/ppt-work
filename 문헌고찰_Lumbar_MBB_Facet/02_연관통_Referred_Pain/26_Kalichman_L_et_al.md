## 이 문헌에서 인용된 근거 (1건)

### [보통] WHICH LEVELS ARE MOST COMMONLY SYMPTOMATIC — L4/5 then L5/S1; degeneration prevalence 45.1% at L4-L5 and 38.2% at L5-S1 in a community population, but degeneration on imaging did NOT correlate with pain.

Clinical consensus across reviews: the commonest site of lumbar facet pain is L4-L5, followed by L5-S1; this is also where standard three-level medial branch blocks are targeted (L3 MB, L4 MB, L5 dorsal ramus, covering L3/4, L4/5 and L5/S1). Community-based imaging prevalence of facet OA: 45.1% at L4-L5 and 38.2% at L5-S1 — BUT in that same community population, facet joint OA at any level showed NO association with low back pain. Biomechanical rationale for the lower-lumbar predominance: greatest compressive load, plus more coronally oriented / variable facet angles at L4-S1 (facet angle range ~30–90 degrees at distal segments) giving LESS resistance to torsional and shear loading; greater sagittal orientation is associated with degenerative spondylolisthesis and with facet degeneration. CAUTION for the deck: the imaging-prevalence figures are OA prevalence, NOT the proportion of symptomatic (block-confirmed) levels — do not present them as the latter.
