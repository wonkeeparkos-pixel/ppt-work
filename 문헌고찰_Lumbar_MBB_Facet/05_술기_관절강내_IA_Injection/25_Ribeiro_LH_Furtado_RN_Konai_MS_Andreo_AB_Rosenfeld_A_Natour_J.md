### [미확인] RIBEIRO 2013 (Spine): RCT of IA facet injection versus SYSTEMIC (intramuscular) steroid — I can describe the design but CANNOT confirm which arm won.

Design as recalled: approximately 60 patients with chronic low back pain and a clinical/imaging diagnosis of facet joint syndrome, randomized to image-guided INTRA-ARTICULAR facet injection with triamcinolone hexacetonide (into up to four joints) versus the same steroid given SYSTEMICALLY by intramuscular injection, with blinded assessment at 1, 3 and 6 months using VAS, Roland-Morris and quality-of-life measures. The design is important because it is the only landmark trial that isolates the SITE of steroid delivery from the systemic pharmacological effect of steroid — i.e. it tests whether putting the steroid IN THE JOINT adds anything. I DO NOT RELIABLY RECALL THE DIRECTION OF THE RESULT and will not guess: it is variously summarized as showing superiority of the intra-articular route on some outcomes at some timepoints, and as showing no meaningful advantage over systemic steroid. THIS MUST BE READ BEFORE IT GOES ON A SLIDE — it is exactly the kind of claim that flips an audience's conclusion.
