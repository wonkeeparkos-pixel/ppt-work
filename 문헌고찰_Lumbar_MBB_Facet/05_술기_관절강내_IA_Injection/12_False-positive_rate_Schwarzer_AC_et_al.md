## 이 문헌에서 인용된 근거 (1건)

### [보통] The capacity limit is the reason IA blocks are NOT accepted as the reference-standard diagnostic test for facet pain — medial branch blocks are.

Because a >1-2 mL injectate escapes into epidural space, foramen, and muscle, an IA block anesthetizes structures other than the joint and cannot be interpreted as target-specific. This, plus the technical difficulty of entering degenerated joints, is why society documents specify MEDIAL BRANCH BLOCKS (with a small, capped volume of ~0.3-0.5 mL per branch) as the validated diagnostic tool and treat IA injection as a therapeutic-only, non-validated diagnostic manoeuvre. Related quantitative context for the deck: the false-positive rate of SINGLE uncontrolled lumbar zygapophysial blocks has been reported at approximately 38%, which is the number behind the dual/comparative-block requirement in guidelines. Reported prevalence of lumbar zygapophysial joint pain in chronic low back pain populations spans roughly 15-45% depending on the block criteria and population.
