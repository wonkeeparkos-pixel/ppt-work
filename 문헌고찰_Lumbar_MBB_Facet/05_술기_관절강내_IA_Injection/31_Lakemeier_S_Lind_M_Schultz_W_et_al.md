## 이 문헌에서 인용된 근거 (2건)

### [보통] LAKEMEIER 2013 (Anesthesia & Analgesia): randomized double-blind IA facet steroid vs radiofrequency denervation — NO significant difference at 6 months.

Design: randomized, double-blinded, controlled trial in approximately 56 patients with chronic low back pain and a positive diagnostic medial branch block, allocated to lumbar facet RADIOFREQUENCY DENERVATION (roughly n=29) versus INTRA-ARTICULAR facet steroid injection (roughly n=27), with 6-month follow-up using Oswestry Disability Index and VAS. Result: both groups improved from baseline and there was NO statistically significant difference between radiofrequency denervation and intra-articular steroid injection at 6 months. This is the head-to-head most often cited by proponents of intra-articular injection, because it fails to demonstrate superiority for the more invasive and more expensive procedure. Standard criticisms: small sample and consequent underpowering for a non-inferiority claim, single (not dual comparative) diagnostic blocks, and a 6-month horizon that may be too short to capture the durability advantage RFA is claimed to have. ### [보통] Bottom line on IA vs MBB: no randomized comparison has shown intra-articular injection to be superior; the two are equivalent in the trials that exist, and MBB wins on feasibility.

Synthesis across Nash 1990 (n≈67), Marks 1992 (n=86) and, for the ablative comparator, Lakemeier 2013 (n≈56): every randomized head-to-head has been NEGATIVE for superiority in either direction, with equivalent modest improvement in both arms. The tiebreakers are therefore practical, and all favor the medial branch block: (a) MBB targets a bony landmark (the SAP/transverse-process junction, and the sacral ala for L5 dorsal ramus) that is present regardless of joint degeneration, whereas the IA route fails in ankylosed/osteophytic joints; (b) MBB requires no arthrogram and no capacity-limited volume; (c) MBB has an accepted diagnostic validation literature and is the required precursor to radiofrequency neurotomy, so it does double duty; (d) MBB is faster and technically easier at L5-S1. The counterargument to present fairly: MBB denervates but does nothing to the synovium, so it cannot address an inflammatory or effusive joint.
