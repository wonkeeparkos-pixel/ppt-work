## 이 문헌에서 인용된 근거 (1건)

### [미확인] Commonly quoted mean facet angles from the sagittal plane: roughly 25-30° at L1-2 rising to roughly 50-60° at L5-S1 — but I could not verify these numbers.

The figures usually reproduced in review articles are approximately: L1-2 ~25-30°, L2-3 ~30-35°, L3-4 ~40-45°, L4-5 ~45-50°, L5-S1 ~50-60° (some series quote up to 65-70° at L5-S1). Series differ substantially depending on whether the angle is measured to the midsagittal plane or to the coronal plane, whether it is measured on dry skeletons vs CT vs MRI, and at which axial slice through the (curved) joint the measurement is taken — this last point alone can shift a single joint's reported angle by 15-20°.
