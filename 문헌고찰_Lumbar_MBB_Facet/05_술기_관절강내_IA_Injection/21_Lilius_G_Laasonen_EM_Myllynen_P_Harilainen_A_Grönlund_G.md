## 이 문헌에서 인용된 근거 (2건)

### [보통] LILIUS 1989 (JBJS Br): three-arm RCT — IA steroid+LA vs PERICAPSULAR steroid+LA vs IA saline — found no difference between arms.

Approximately 109 patients with chronic low back pain randomized to three groups: (a) cortisone plus local anesthetic INTO the facet joints, (b) cortisone plus local anesthetic AROUND the joints (pericapsular), (c) physiological SALINE into the joints. All three groups improved and there were no statistically significant differences between them at follow-up. The authors' inference — the one worth putting on a slide — is that the benefit is attributable neither to the steroid nor to the intra-articular location, which points to a large non-specific/placebo or needling effect. MAJOR CRITICISM: the injected volume was reportedly very large (on the order of 8 mL per site in the published protocol), i.e. many times the 1-2 mL joint capacity, so the 'intra-articular' arm was necessarily extra-articular — which arguably explains why the intra- and peri-capsular arms behaved identically. Patients were also selected without controlled blocks. VOLUME FIGURE IS RECALLED AND SHOULD BE VERIFIED.

*원 출처 표기*: `Lilius G, Laasonen EM, Myllynen P, Harilainen A, Grönlund G. Journal of Bone and Joint Surgery (British volume), 1989`

### [보통] Duration of relief after IA steroid is best described as weeks to about 3 months, with wide variance and no reliable predictor.

Across the trial literature the honest summary is: onset within days; meaningful relief commonly measured in WEEKS TO ~3 MONTHS; a minority report longer. Carette's raw 6-month difference is the outlier that did not survive adjustment. Because Lilius found intra-articular saline as effective as intra-articular steroid, a substantial part of the observed duration in uncontrolled practice is non-specific. For the deck, avoid quoting a single mean duration — quote the range and note that the trials that measured it were selecting patients with uncontrolled single blocks, which inflates the placebo-responder fraction.

*원 출처 표기*: `Synthesis of Carette S et al., New England Journal of Medicine, 1991; Lilius G et al., Journal of Bone and Joint Surgery (Br), 1989; Marks RC et al., Pain, 1992`
