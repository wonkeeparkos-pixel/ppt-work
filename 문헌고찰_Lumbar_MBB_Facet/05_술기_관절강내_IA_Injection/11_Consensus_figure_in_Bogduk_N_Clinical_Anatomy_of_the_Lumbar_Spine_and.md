## 이 문헌에서 인용된 근거 (1건)

### [보통] JOINT CAPACITY: the lumbar zygapophysial joint holds approximately 1-2 mL; most sources give 1-1.5 mL and ISIS/SIS-style guidance caps injection near 1 mL.

The 1-2 mL figure is the number quoted almost universally in interventional and anatomic texts for the normal adult lumbar ZJ. Narrower statements commonly seen: normal capacity 1-1.5 mL; degenerated joints with fibrosis/osteophytosis hold LESS (sometimes <1 mL); joints with capsular laxity, an effusion, or an associated synovial cyst hold MORE. The clinically actionable rule for the deck: keep total intra-articular volume at or below ~1 mL (some allow up to 1.5 mL), counting the contrast already injected, because beyond capacity the capsule ruptures and the injection is no longer intra-articular. I could NOT verify the primary cadaveric/arthrographic measurement papers or their exact mean capacity with confidence intervals — the 1-2 mL range is the recalled consensus figure, not a value I read.
