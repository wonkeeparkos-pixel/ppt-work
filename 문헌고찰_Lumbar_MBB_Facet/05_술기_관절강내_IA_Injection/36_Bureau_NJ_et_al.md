### [미확인] Special indication 1 — JUXTAFACET (SYNOVIAL) CYST: the intra-articular route is the only percutaneous route that can distend and rupture the cyst.

A lumbar synovial cyst arises from the facet joint capsule and usually communicates with the joint, so contrast/injectate delivered intra-articularly can be driven into the cyst under pressure to distend and RUPTURE it, decompressing the lateral recess; the cyst may alternatively or additionally be punctured directly and aspirated. A medial branch block or radiofrequency neurotomy can do neither. Reported outcomes: percutaneous rupture avoids subsequent surgery in a substantial minority-to-majority of patients (figures in the range of roughly 50-75% avoiding surgery are commonly quoted), with recurrence and eventual surgery in the remainder; the key cited series is Martha JF et al., The Spine Journal, 2009 (a case series of approximately 101 patients undergoing percutaneous rupture of lumbar synovial cysts), and earlier imaging-guided steroid-plus-distension work by Bureau NJ et al., Radiology, 2001. There is also literature on whether cyst T2 signal intensity predicts rupture success. ALL PERCENTAGES HERE ARE RECALLED AND UNVERIFIED.
