What I can place with reasonable confidence in that period is a different design: Manchikanti L, Singh V, Falco FJE, Cash KA, Pampati V, published in International Journal of Medical Sciences in 2010, a randomized double-blind controlled trial of therapeutic lumbar FACET JOINT NERVE BLOCKS (medial branch blocks) with 2-year follow-up, in which the randomization was local anesthetic ALONE versus local anesthetic PLUS STEROID — NOT medial branch block versus intra-articular injection.
