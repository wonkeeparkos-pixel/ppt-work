### [미확인] NASS and Spine Intervention Society positions are more restrictive than ASIPP: IA facet injection is generally NOT recommended as a therapeutic intervention.

The North American Spine Society's coverage-policy recommendations for lumbar facet interventions are conventionally cited as NOT supporting intra-articular facet joint injections for chronic low back pain, while supporting diagnostic medial branch blocks (with defined relief thresholds and block counts) as the gateway to radiofrequency neurotomy. The Spine Intervention Society position (expressed in its Practice Guidelines and in its FactFinders series) is likewise that medial branch blocks are the validated diagnostic instrument and that intra-articular injection has no established therapeutic role. NET TEACHING POINT — there is a genuine, citable SOCIETY-LEVEL DISAGREEMENT: interventional pain societies grade IA injection as weak-but-nonzero evidence, whereas spine-surgical/coverage bodies and evidence-based primary-care guidelines (e.g. the American Pain Society / American College of Physicians line) grade it as not recommended. Present both.
