### [보통] The 2020 multispecialty international consensus guideline on lumbar facet joint pain is the best single modern reference for a balanced verdict.

Cohen SP, Bhaskar A, Bhatia A, et al., published in Regional Anesthesia and Pain Medicine in 2020, is a multispecialty international working-group consensus on interventions for lumbar facet joint pain, co-developed across pain-medicine societies. It is the document to cite for a modern, non-partisan position on selection criteria (block thresholds, number of blocks), on the role of imaging findings, and on where intra-articular injection sits relative to medial branch blocks and radiofrequency ablation. As recalled, it does not endorse routine therapeutic intra-articular injection while acknowledging selected indications. I could not retrieve the document to quote its graded recommendations verbatim.
