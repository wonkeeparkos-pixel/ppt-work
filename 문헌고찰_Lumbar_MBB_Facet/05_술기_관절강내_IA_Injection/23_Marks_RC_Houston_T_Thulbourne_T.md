## 이 문헌에서 인용된 근거 (3건)

### [보통] MARKS 1992 (Pain): randomized IA facet injection vs facet nerve block in 86 patients — no significant difference between techniques; neither gave substantial lasting relief.

Design: 86 patients with chronic low back pain randomized to intra-articular facet joint injection versus facet (medial branch) nerve block, using comparable drug (methylprednisolone plus local anesthetic), with follow-up at 1, 3 and 6 months. Result: modest improvement in both arms with no statistically significant difference between the two techniques at any timepoint; a trend toward greater relief in the nerve-block arm at the 1-month point is commonly quoted. Conclusion: neither technique produced substantial long-term benefit, and the choice between them cannot be made on efficacy grounds. Together with Nash 1990 this is the core of the 'IA is not superior to MBB' teaching point. TIMEPOINT-SPECIFIC NUMBERS UNVERIFIED.

*원 출처 표기*: `Marks RC, Houston T, Thulbourne T. Pain, 1992`

### [보통] Bottom line on IA vs MBB: no randomized comparison has shown intra-articular injection to be superior; the two are equivalent in the trials that exist, and MBB wins on feasibility.

Synthesis across Nash 1990 (n≈67), Marks 1992 (n=86) and, for the ablative comparator, Lakemeier 2013 (n≈56): every randomized head-to-head has been NEGATIVE for superiority in either direction, with equivalent modest improvement in both arms. The tiebreakers are therefore practical, and all favor the medial branch block: (a) MBB targets a bony landmark (the SAP/transverse-process junction, and the sacral ala for L5 dorsal ramus) that is present regardless of joint degeneration, whereas the IA route fails in ankylosed/osteophytic joints; (b) MBB requires no arthrogram and no capacity-limited volume; (c) MBB has an accepted diagnostic validation literature and is the required precursor to radiofrequency neurotomy, so it does double duty; (d) MBB is faster and technically easier at L5-S1. The counterargument to present fairly: MBB denervates but does nothing to the synovium, so it cannot address an inflammatory or effusive joint.

*원 출처 표기*: `Synthesis of Nash TP, The Pain Clinic, 1990; Marks RC et al., Pain, 1992; Lakemeier S et al., Anesthesia & Analgesia, 2013`

### [보통] Duration of relief after IA steroid is best described as weeks to about 3 months, with wide variance and no reliable predictor.

Across the trial literature the honest summary is: onset within days; meaningful relief commonly measured in WEEKS TO ~3 MONTHS; a minority report longer. Carette's raw 6-month difference is the outlier that did not survive adjustment. Because Lilius found intra-articular saline as effective as intra-articular steroid, a substantial part of the observed duration in uncontrolled practice is non-specific. For the deck, avoid quoting a single mean duration — quote the range and note that the trials that measured it were selecting patients with uncontrolled single blocks, which inflates the placebo-responder fraction.

*원 출처 표기*: `Synthesis of Carette S et al., New England Journal of Medicine, 1991; Lilius G et al., Journal of Bone and Joint Surgery (Br), 1989; Marks RC et al., Pain, 1992`
