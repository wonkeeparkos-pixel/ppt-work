## 이 문헌에서 인용된 근거 (1건)

### [미확인] ASIPP 2020 facet guidelines rate therapeutic IA lumbar facet injections BELOW medial branch blocks and radiofrequency neurotomy — but I could not verify the exact level letters.

The relevant document is the comprehensive evidence-based ASIPP guideline for facet joint interventions published in Pain Physician in 2020 (Manchikanti L, Kaye AD, Soin A, et al.), a supplement-length document covering cervical, thoracic and lumbar facet interventions. The DIRECTION of its verdicts is not in doubt: diagnostic lumbar medial branch blocks with dual/comparative technique and an ≥80% relief threshold, therapeutic lumbar medial branch blocks, and lumbar radiofrequency neurotomy all receive the STRONGER evidence ratings (Level II / moderate, in ASIPP's own I-V scheme), whereas therapeutic lumbar INTRA-ARTICULAR facet injections receive a WEAKER rating (Level III 'fair' or Level IV 'limited' — earlier ASIPP guideline iterations, e.g. the 2013 Pain Physician guidelines, assigned lumbar IA injections limited evidence). I CANNOT CONFIRM WHETHER THE 2020 DOCUMENT SAYS LEVEL III OR LEVEL IV for lumbar IA injections. Look this up before assigning a level on a slide — it is a frequently misquoted line.
