## 이 문헌에서 인용된 근거 (1건)

### [미확인] Complication profile is favorable but the capsular-rupture mechanism drives the notable ones.

Reported adverse events, all uncommon: infection (facet septic arthritis, paraspinal abscess, and — via the ruptured superior recess pathway — epidural abscess); transient radicular symptoms or unexpected segmental/epidural anesthesia when injectate escapes into the epidural space or foramen; dural puncture and, very rarely, inadvertent subarachnoid injection producing high spinal block; steroid class effects (facial flushing, transient hyperglycemia — clinically relevant in diabetics, HPA-axis suppression with repetition, fluid retention); vasovagal reaction; and transient increase in pain from capsular distension. Vascular uptake within the facet joint itself is uncommon compared with epidural or cervical procedures. I could not verify any incidence figures — do not put a complication rate on a slide from these notes.
