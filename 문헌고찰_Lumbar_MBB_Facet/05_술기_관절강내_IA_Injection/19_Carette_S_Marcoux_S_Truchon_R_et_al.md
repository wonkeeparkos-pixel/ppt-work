## 이 문헌에서 인용된 근거 (1건)

### [보통] CARETTE 1991 (NEJM) is the landmark negative RCT: IA methylprednisolone was 'of little value' for chronic LBP.

Design: double-blind randomized trial. Patients with chronic low back pain were first screened with a LOCAL-ANESTHETIC-ONLY intra-articular facet injection; those reporting at least 50% relief were randomized to intra-articular methylprednisolone acetate (20 mg) versus isotonic saline, injected under fluoroscopic guidance into the two lowest facet joints. Approximately 97 patients were randomized (roughly 49 steroid vs 48 saline) out of roughly 190 screened. Outcome: at 1 month there was NO significant difference in the proportion reporting marked or very marked improvement (roughly 42% steroid vs 33% placebo). At 6 months the raw proportions favored steroid (roughly 46% vs 15%), but after adjustment for co-interventions and other prognostic variables the difference lost significance. Authors' conclusion as written: injecting methylprednisolone acetate into the facet joints is of little value in the treatment of patients with chronic low back pain.
