## 이 문헌에서 인용된 근거 (2건)

### [보통] A correct lumbar facet arthrogram appears with only about 0.1-0.3 mL of contrast and outlines an elongated joint with a superior and an inferior recess.

Expected appearance: a thin, sharply marginated, elongated (bean-, comma-, or dumbbell-shaped) collection that follows the curve of the joint, with contrast filling and bulging into the superior and inferior subcapsular recesses so the image looks bulbous at both poles and narrow in between; the contrast must remain confined and must NOT (a) run linearly cephalocaudad along the ventral surface of the lamina (epidural), (b) track laterally into the intervertebral foramen outlining a nerve root sleeve, (c) form a feathery/striated pattern in muscle, or (d) wash out instantly with a vascular pattern. Volume: the arthrogram is normally established with roughly 0.1-0.3 mL of nonionic contrast (iohexol/iopamidol); if 0.5 mL has gone in without an arthrogram appearing, the needle is almost certainly extra-articular. Total injectate (contrast + steroid + local anesthetic) is conventionally capped at about 1-1.5 mL per joint because of the capacity limit below.

*원 출처 표기*: `Facet arthrography descriptions — Dory MA, Radiology, 1981; Destouet JM et al., Radiology, 1982; volumes as codified in ISIS/SIS Practice Guidelines (Bogduk N, ed.)`

### [보통] Above capacity the capsule ruptures along four described escape routes: epidural space, intervertebral foramen, interlaminar/interspinous space, and paravertebral muscle.

Described patterns of contrast escape on overdistension arthrography: (1) SUPERIOR recess rupture → contrast into the EPIDURAL space (the superior recess is separated from the epidural fat only by the thin ventral capsule/ligamentum flavum) and, continuing laterally, into the INTERVERTEBRAL FORAMEN where it can outline the exiting nerve root sleeve — this is the route that produces a radicular pattern of relief after a supposedly 'intra-articular' injection; (2) INFERIOR recess rupture → contrast into the INTERLAMINAR/interspinous space and into the PARAVERTEBRAL (multifidus) MUSCULATURE; (3) direct posterior capsular rupture → paraspinal muscle only. Reported clinical consequences of overdistension: reproduction of the patient's pain by distension alone (used deliberately in provocation arthrography), transient radicular symptoms, and — the key teaching point — loss of diagnostic specificity, because an injectate that has reached the epidural space or a nerve root can relieve pain of non-facet origin. A commonly cited additional point is that L5-S1 shows the greatest tendency to communicate with the epidural/sacral epidural space.
