### [미확인] FUCHS 2005 (JVIR): CT-guided IA hyaluronic acid vs IA glucocorticoid in lumbar facet OA — both effective, steroid faster, HA more durable.

Design: prospective randomized comparison in approximately 60 patients with non-radicular low back pain attributed to lumbar facet osteoarthritis, using CT-guided intra-articular injection of sodium hyaluronate versus a corticosteroid (triamcinolone), with follow-up to about 6 months and outcomes including VAS pain, Roland-Morris disability, Oswestry disability and SF-36 quality of life. Result as usually summarized: BOTH arms improved significantly from baseline across pain, function and quality-of-life domains; the corticosteroid arm improved faster (early timepoints), while the hyaluronic acid arm caught up and was at least as good, and by some accounts better maintained, at the later (3-6 month) timepoints. This is one of the few positive IA facet trials and is the standard citation for viscosupplementation of the facet joint. SAMPLE SIZE, DOSING SCHEDULE (single vs series of weekly injections) AND THE STATISTICAL DIRECTION AT EACH TIMEPOINT ARE RECALLED AND UNVERIFIED.
