### [보통] COCHRANE verdict: insufficient evidence to support injection therapy, including facet joint injections, for subacute/chronic low back pain.

The Cochrane review of injection therapy for subacute and chronic low back pain (Staal JB, de Bie R, de Vet HCW, Hildebrandt J, Nelemans P) concluded that there is insufficient evidence to support the use of injection therapy in subacute and chronic low back pain, while explicitly noting that the reviewers could not rule out benefit in specific subgroups, and criticizing the primary trials for heterogeneous patient selection, small samples, and inadequate diagnostic criteria. The facet-specific trials pooled were essentially Carette, Lilius, Marks/Nash and similar. This 'insufficient evidence / cannot exclude subgroup benefit' formulation is the phrasing to quote — it is NOT the same as 'proven ineffective', and the distinction is the crux of the ongoing dispute with interventional societies.
