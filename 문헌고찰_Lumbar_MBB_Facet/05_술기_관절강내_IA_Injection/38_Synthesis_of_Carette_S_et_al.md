## 이 문헌에서 인용된 근거 (1건)

### [보통] Duration of relief after IA steroid is best described as weeks to about 3 months, with wide variance and no reliable predictor.

Across the trial literature the honest summary is: onset within days; meaningful relief commonly measured in WEEKS TO ~3 MONTHS; a minority report longer. Carette's raw 6-month difference is the outlier that did not survive adjustment. Because Lilius found intra-articular saline as effective as intra-articular steroid, a substantial part of the observed duration in uncontrolled practice is non-specific. For the deck, avoid quoting a single mean duration — quote the range and note that the trials that measured it were selecting patients with uncontrolled single blocks, which inflates the placebo-responder fraction.
