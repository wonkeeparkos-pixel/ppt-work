## 이 문헌에서 인용된 근거 (1건)

### [미확인] Guideline repeat-injection rules (ASIPP-style): repeat only if ≥50% relief was obtained and lasted long enough, minimum ~2 months apart, roughly 4-6 procedures per region per year.

The framework consistently applied in ASIPP guidance for THERAPEUTIC facet interventions: (1) a therapeutic block is repeated only when the prior block produced at least ~50% relief of adequate duration (commonly stated as 6-8 weeks, with ≥3 months of relief the target for a procedure to be considered worth continuing); (2) a minimum interval of about 2 months between injections in the same region; (3) an annual cap of roughly 4-6 procedures per region; (4) DIAGNOSTIC blocks are capped at two (dual comparative) per level and are not repeated indefinitely. Drug/volume parameters conventionally used: methylprednisolone 20-40 mg, or triamcinolone 10-20 mg, or betamethasone 3-6 mg, or dexamethasone 4 mg, mixed with local anesthetic to a TOTAL of ≤1-1.5 mL per joint including the contrast already injected — the volume cap follows directly from joint capacity. THE EXACT NUMERIC CAPS AND INTERVALS DIFFER BETWEEN GUIDELINE EDITIONS AND PAYERS AND MUST BE VERIFIED AGAINST THE CURRENT DOCUMENT.
