## 이 문헌에서 인용된 근거 (1건)

### [확실] Carette's criticisms — patient selection is the central one, and it is the criticism the deck should lead with.

(1) SELECTION: entry required only a SINGLE, uncontrolled local-anesthetic block with a ≥50% relief threshold. Both elements are now known to be inadequate — single uncontrolled lumbar facet blocks carry a false-positive rate around 38%, and the accepted threshold has moved to ≥80% relief with dual comparative blocks. A substantial fraction of Carette's randomized population therefore probably did not have facet-mediated pain, biasing toward the null. (2) 'PLACEBO' CHOICE: intra-articular normal saline is not inert — it distends and lavages the joint and may itself be therapeutic, again biasing toward the null. (3) FIXED TARGET: the two LOWEST joints were injected regardless of which level the screening block implicated. (4) The 6-month signal favoring steroid was discounted on the basis of co-interventions, which some commentators regard as over-correction of a real effect. (5) Volume/spread was not controlled, so an unknown proportion of injections may have exceeded capacity and become epidural.
