### [보통] Ultrasound-guided IA lumbar facet injection: paramedian sagittal 'camel hump'/'saw-tooth' view of the articular processes with the joint as the hypoechoic cleft between humps.

Technique: prone, curvilinear low-frequency probe (2-5 MHz). Start transverse midline to identify the spinous process, then slide laterally to the lamina and the articular process line; rotate to a PARAMEDIAN SAGITTAL plane over the articular pillars, where the superior and inferior articular processes give a continuous undulating 'camel hump' (saw-tooth) contour with the joint cleft as the hypoechoic notch at the trough between adjacent humps. Count levels from the sacrum (flat sacral surface) upward. Needle in-plane from caudad to cephalad, or out-of-plane down the trough, into the cleft. Advantages: no radiation, real-time soft-tissue and vessel visualization, portable, useful in pregnancy. Fundamental limitation: ultrasound CANNOT confirm intra-articular placement — there is no arthrogram — so the endpoint is sonographic tip position plus tactile feel only.
