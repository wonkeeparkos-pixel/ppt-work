### [미확인] NASH 1990 (The Pain Clinic): randomized IA facet steroid vs medial branch block — no significant difference, with a nonsignificant trend favoring the nerve block.

A randomized comparison of intra-articular facet injection against facet (medial branch) nerve block in roughly 67 patients. Both groups improved; the medial branch group did numerically slightly better but the difference did not reach significance. The practical conclusion drawn at the time and repeated since: medial branch blocks are technically easier, faster, need no arthrogram, work in degenerated joints, and are no worse — so there is no efficacy argument for the harder procedure. This paper is in a low-visibility journal (The Pain Clinic) and I could NOT verify the sample size, the outcome timepoints, or the exact journal volume/pages.
