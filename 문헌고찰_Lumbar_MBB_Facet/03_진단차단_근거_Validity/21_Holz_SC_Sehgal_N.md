## 이 문헌에서 인용된 근거 (1건)

### [확실] Against the threshold orthodoxy: Holz & Sehgal found NO correlation between MBB response and RFN outcome.

Retrospective analysis of 112 patients with lumbar and cervical pain (28% cervical), 3-month follow-up after radiofrequency neurotomy, mean 48% pain relief. No correlation was seen between medial branch block results and pain relief after RFN. The one signal: individuals with 100% relief from lidocaine lasting more than 8 hours responded best. Their dual-MBB positivity cut-off was 70%. Authors conclude it remains unclear how many blocks are needed and what MBB criteria should be used before RFN.
