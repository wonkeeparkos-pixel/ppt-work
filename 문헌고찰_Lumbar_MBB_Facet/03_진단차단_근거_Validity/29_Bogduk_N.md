## 이 문헌에서 인용된 근거 (1건)

### [미확인] Bogduk's defence of the paradigm is a discrete, citable counterweight to the cost-effectiveness argument.

Bogduk N, 'On diagnostic blocks for lumbar zygapophysial joint pain,' F1000 Med Rep 2010;2:57 (PubMed record PMID 21173871) is the standard rebuttal reference arguing for controlled blocks and a high relief threshold. I was unable to retrieve its text this session, so I cannot quote its specific arguments or numbers. Similarly, the frequently cited RFA-validation study selecting patients by rigorous dual blocks — Dreyfuss P, Halbrook B, Pauza K, Joshi A, McLarty J, Bogduk N, 'Efficacy and validity of radiofrequency neurotomy for chronic lumbar zygapophysial joint pain,' Spine 2000;25(10):1270-7 — is commonly quoted as showing 60% of patients with >=90% relief and 87% with >=60% relief at 12 months; those two figures are from recall and were NOT verified in this session.
