## 이 문헌에서 인용된 근거 (1건)

### [확실] Prospectively measured epidural spread during lumbar IA facet injection is far higher than most clinicians assume: 64.6% of patients, 49.5% of procedures.

100 consecutive patients (38 men, 62 women; mean age 68 y, range 20-88) undergoing lumbar facet joint injection at two sites, April-June 2014. Epidural spread occurred in 64.6% of patients (64/99) and 49.5% of procedures (95/192). Ventral spread 29.2%; foraminal spread 18.8%. This is the strongest single dataset undermining the target-specificity of IA injection as a diagnostic test — roughly half of all IA injections also bathe the epidural space.
