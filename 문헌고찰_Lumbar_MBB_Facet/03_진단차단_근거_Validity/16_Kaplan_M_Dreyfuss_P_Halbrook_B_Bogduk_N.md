## 이 문헌에서 인용된 근거 (1건)

### [확실] Kaplan 1998 physiologic challenge: a properly performed lumbar MBB anesthetized the zygapophysial joint in 89% of cases (8 of 9).

18 asymptomatic volunteers randomized to L4-L5 or L5-S1 intra-articular contrast injection until capsular distension elicited pain WITHOUT extracapsular contrast spread (establishing a reproducible nociceptive stimulus of known origin). One week later, 15 blinded volunteers received randomized saline or 2% lidocaine medial branch injections, then repeat capsular distension. Of the 9 who received 2% lidocaine MBB, 8 felt no pain and 1 felt pain on repeat distension = 89% success (i.e., an ~11% false-NEGATIVE rate for a technically correct MBB). There was a significant effect of 2% lidocaine vs saline on anesthetizing the joint when venous uptake was avoided. Note the small denominator (n=9) — the 89% has wide uncertainty.
