## 이 문헌에서 인용된 근거 (1건)

### [확실] Manchikanti's pooled figure across controlled comparative-block studies: 21-41% (average 31%); the wider literature range quoted is 15-45%.

Recurring summary statement across Manchikanti's reviews and guidelines: 'lumbar facet joints have been shown as the source of chronic pain in 21% to 41% of low back pain patients, with an average prevalence of 31%, utilizing controlled comparative local anesthetic blocks.' A parallel, broader statement in the same literature is 'facet joints implicated in 15% to 45% of chronic LBP using controlled diagnostic blocks in accordance with IASP criteria.' The ASRA 2024 review restates the range as 15-45%.
