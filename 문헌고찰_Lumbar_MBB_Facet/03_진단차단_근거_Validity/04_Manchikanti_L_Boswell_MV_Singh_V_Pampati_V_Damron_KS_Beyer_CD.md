## 이 문헌에서 인용된 근거 (2건)

### [보통] Manchikanti 2004: 500 consecutive patients, controlled comparative blocks — cervical 55%, thoracic 42%; lumbar figure not retrievable in this session.

500 consecutive patients with chronic non-specific spinal pain. Controlled comparative local anesthetic blocks using 1% lidocaine, then 0.25% bupivacaine in lidocaine-responders, per IASP criteria. Verified: cervical facet joint pain prevalence 55% (95% CI 49-61%); thoracic 42% (95% CI 30-53%). The lumbar prevalence and false-positive figures (widely quoted as ~31% prevalence with ~27% false-positive rate) could NOT be verified from retrievable text — treat the 31%/27% pair as unconfirmed until the full text is checked.

*원 출처 표기*: `Manchikanti L, Boswell MV, Singh V, Pampati V, Damron KS, Beyer CD. BMC Musculoskelet Disord 2004;5:15. PMID 15169547. doi:10.1186/1471-2474-5-15`

### [확실] The comparative-block design substitutes a pharmacologic discriminator (duration) for a placebo arm: lidocaine first, bupivacaine second, positive only if relief is concordant and longer with the long-acting agent.

Standard Manchikanti protocol: 1% lidocaine as the screening block; responders re-blocked with 0.25% bupivacaine; positive = concordant relief on both, with the bupivacaine block producing longer relief, per IASP criteria. Schwarzer's protocol used lignocaine then 0.5% bupivacaine with a >=50% relief threshold on the confirmatory block. The design's advantage is ethical/practical feasibility (no deception, no saline arm); its acknowledged weakness is that a patient with a durable placebo response, or one who simply reports what is expected, can still pass both blocks.

*원 출처 표기*: `Manchikanti L et al. BMC Musculoskelet Disord 2004;5:15 (PMID 15169547); Schwarzer AC et al. Pain 1994;58(2):195-200 (PMID 7816487)`
