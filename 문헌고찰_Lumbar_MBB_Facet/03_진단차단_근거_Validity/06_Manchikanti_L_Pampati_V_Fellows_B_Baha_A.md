## 이 문헌에서 인용된 근거 (1건)

### [확실] Elderly (>=65 y) have roughly double the facet-pain prevalence of younger adults: 52% vs 30%, with higher false-positive rates too (33% vs 26%).

Controlled comparative prevalence study, 100 patients: Group I n=50 aged <=64 y; Group II n=50 aged >=65 y. Prevalence of facet-joint-mediated pain 30% in adults vs 52% in the elderly (significantly higher). False-positive rate of the single screening block 26% in adults vs 33% in the elderly.
