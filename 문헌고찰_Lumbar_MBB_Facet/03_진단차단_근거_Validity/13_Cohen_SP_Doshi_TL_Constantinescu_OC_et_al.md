## 이 문헌에서 인용된 근거 (3건)

### [확실] Mechanisms of single-block over-diagnosis: placebo response, non-specific spread of injectate, sedation, and expectancy/reporting bias.

The four evidence-anchored contributors: (1) placebo response — 24% positive-responder rate in the saline arm of FACTS at 3 months (Cohen 2018); (2) non-target spread — 0.5 mL of contrast reached the epidural space and/or intervertebral foramen in 16% of MBB injections on CT in healthy volunteers (Dreyfuss 1997), and 0.5 mL in cadavers reaches adjacent-level medial branches (Wahezi 2018), so relief may come from anesthetizing a different generator; (3) concomitant sedation/systemic uptake; (4) regression to the mean and patient expectancy. Note that mechanisms (1) and (4) are NOT controlled by a comparative (lidocaine-then-bupivacaine) design — only by a true placebo arm.

*원 출처 표기*: `Synthesis of Dreyfuss P et al. Spine 1997;22(8):895-902; Wahezi SE et al. PM R 2018;10(6):616-22 (PMID 29174073); Cohen SP et al. Anesthesiology 2018;129(3):517-35 (PMID 29847426)`

### [확실] Best available placebo-response anchor in this population: 24% at 3 months in a randomized saline arm.

FACTS trial: 229 participants randomized 2:2:1 to intra-articular facet injection with bupivacaine + steroid, medial branch blocks, or saline. At 3 months the proportion of positive responders was 51% (intra-articular), 56% (medial branch block), and 24% (placebo/saline) (P = 0.005). Positive outcome defined as >=2-point decrease in average back pain score plus satisfaction score >=3/5. This 24% is a defensible teaching number for the placebo/nonspecific response rate against which any single uncontrolled block must be discounted. Separately, the block-positivity criterion used to advance patients in FACTS was >=50% relief sustained for at least 3 hours.

*원 출처 표기*: `Cohen SP, Doshi TL, Constantinescu OC, et al. 'Effectiveness of Lumbar Facet Joint Blocks and Predictive Value before Radiofrequency Denervation: The Facet Treatment Study (FACTS), a Randomized, Controlled Clinical Trial.' Anesthesiology 2018;129(3):517-35. doi:10.1097/ALN.0000000000002274. PMID 29847426`

### [확실] FACTS also established that facet blocks are not therapeutic — average pain scores at 3 months did not differ between arms.

Mean NRS pain at 3 months: 3.0 +/- 2.0 intra-articular, 3.2 +/- 2.5 medial branch, 3.5 +/- 1.9 control (P = 0.493) — no significant difference. The significant difference was only in the dichotomous responder proportions (51%/56%/24%, P = 0.005). Authors' conclusion: facet blocks are not therapeutic; the higher responder rates generate the hypothesis that blocks provide PROGNOSTIC value before RFA.
