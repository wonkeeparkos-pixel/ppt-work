## 이 문헌에서 인용된 근거 (1건)

### [확실] Age-stratified data show lumbar facet prevalence rising from 18% (age 31-40) to 44% (age 51-60), i.e. a roughly 2.5-fold gradient.

Retrospective analysis of 424 patients divided into 6 age groups (I: 18-30; II: 31-40; III: 41-50; IV: 51-60; V: 61-70; VI: >70), all evaluated with controlled comparative local anesthetic blocks. Lumbar facet joint involvement ranged from 18% (Group II, 31-40 y) to 44% (Group IV, 51-60 y), with high rates persisting in Group V (61-70 y). By contrast, cervical facet prevalence was LOWEST (33%) in the >70 group — the age gradient is not uniform across spinal regions.
