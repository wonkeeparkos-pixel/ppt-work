## 이 문헌에서 인용된 근거 (1건)

### [확실] The 'how many blocks' question is now formally open in the guideline literature and in practice surveys.

The ASRA Pain Medicine News 2024 review by Assavanop and Bhatia is titled 'Evidence for Diagnostic Blocks Prior to Radiofrequency Ablation of Innervation to the Lumbar Facet Joints — None, Once, or Twice?' It frames four unsettled parameters: (1) number of blocks; (2) the level of pain relief considered significant; (3) the injectate used; (4) the timing of outcome assessment — all framed as a trade-off between diagnostic accuracy and cost-effectiveness. It states that two sets of diagnostic MBBs are recommended by the International Pain and Spine Intervention Society and many others as the most reliable method, that MBB should be the prognostic injection of choice before RFA, and that a favourable RFA prognosis is associated with >80% pain reduction after two procedures. A parallel international survey of pain medicine physicians on the same question was published in 2024 (PMID 39029928); I could not retrieve its numbers.
