## 이 문헌에서 인용된 근거 (1건)

### [보통] Durability data favour the 80% threshold: 89.5% of >80%-relief responders still had facet-mediated pain at 2 years vs only 51% of 50%-relief responders.

Reported comparison: of patients who obtained >80% relief from diagnostic medial branch blocks, 89.5% continued to have facet-joint-mediated pain at 2-year follow-up; in the 50%-relief arm, only 51% still had lumbar facet-mediated pain at 2 years. This is the core empirical argument for the 80% criterion — the 50% threshold roughly doubles the rate of diagnoses that do not hold up over time. The primary source is almost certainly Manchikanti L et al., 'Are diagnostic lumbar medial branch blocks valid? Results of 2-year follow-up,' Pain Physician 2006;9(4):313-9 (PMID 16883373), but I could not open that paper to confirm the two percentages map to it.
