### [미확인] Manchikanti's 2020 update reframes the diagnostic problem but its headline numbers should be verified against the full text.

Manchikanti L et al. 'Low Back Pain and Diagnostic Lumbar Facet Joint Nerve Blocks: Assessment of Prevalence, False-Positive Rates, and a Philosophical Paradigm Shift from an Acute to a Chronic Pain Model.' Pain Physician 2020;23(5):519-530. PMID 32967394. Secondary sources retrieved in this session report a prevalence of 34.1% with a false-positive rate of 49.8%. I could not open the primary text to confirm these two numbers or their CIs — verify before putting them on a slide. The stated purpose of the paper is an updated assessment of prevalence and false-positive rates using controlled comparative blocks with concordant pain relief, plus an argument for shifting from an acute-injury model to a chronic-pain model of facet pain.
