### [보통] Across dual-block studies pooled in ASIPP/Manchikanti systematic reviews: prevalence 16-41%, false-positive rate 25-44%.

'17 studies with dual diagnostic blocks showed an average prevalence of 16% to 41% and false-positive rates of 25% to 44%.' A separate summary attributed to the comprehensive ASIPP facet guidelines reports prevalence varying 34%-48% with false-positive rates varying 42%-58% — the two ranges come from different inclusion sets/criteria and are commonly quoted side by side; report both rather than reconciling them.
