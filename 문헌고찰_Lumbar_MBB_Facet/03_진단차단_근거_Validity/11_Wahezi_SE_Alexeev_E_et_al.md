## 이 문헌에서 인용된 근거 (3건)

### [확실] A cadaveric-study review quotes the false-positive rate of diagnostic local anesthetic blocks as 27%-63% and names injectate volume as a contributing cause.

Wahezi et al. frame their volume study with: 'Diagnostic local anesthetic blocks have a false-positive rate of 27%-63%,' and hypothesize injectate volume as a contributor to false positivity. This is the widest range in circulation and is useful for showing the audience that the '25-45%' figure is itself an approximation.

*원 출처 표기*: `Wahezi SE, Alexeev E, et al. 'Lumbar Medial Branch Block Volume-Dependent Dispersion Patterns as a Predictor for Ablation Success: A Cadaveric Study.' PM R 2018;10(6):616-22. PMID 29174073`

### [확실] Mechanisms of single-block over-diagnosis: placebo response, non-specific spread of injectate, sedation, and expectancy/reporting bias.

The four evidence-anchored contributors: (1) placebo response — 24% positive-responder rate in the saline arm of FACTS at 3 months (Cohen 2018); (2) non-target spread — 0.5 mL of contrast reached the epidural space and/or intervertebral foramen in 16% of MBB injections on CT in healthy volunteers (Dreyfuss 1997), and 0.5 mL in cadavers reaches adjacent-level medial branches (Wahezi 2018), so relief may come from anesthetizing a different generator; (3) concomitant sedation/systemic uptake; (4) regression to the mean and patient expectancy. Note that mechanisms (1) and (4) are NOT controlled by a comparative (lidocaine-then-bupivacaine) design — only by a true placebo arm.

*원 출처 표기*: `Synthesis of Dreyfuss P et al. Spine 1997;22(8):895-902; Wahezi SE et al. PM R 2018;10(6):616-22 (PMID 29174073); Cohen SP et al. Anesthesiology 2018;129(3):517-35 (PMID 29847426)`

### [확실] Cadaveric dispersion data argue for 0.25 mL, not 0.5 mL: 0.5 mL blocks adjacent-level medial branches and degrades specificity.

Cadaveric comparison of 0.25 mL vs 0.5 mL injectate at lumbar medial branch targets. Findings: 0.25 mL reliably contacted the lumbar medial branch WITHOUT extensive extravasation; 0.5 mL 'may produce an adjacent-level nerve block in addition to the intended injection level, thus decreasing the specificity of a targeted lumbar medial branch block.' Authors conclude 0.25 mL total volume may provide greater specificity for RFA planning. A companion cervical study by the same group followed (Wahezi et al., PM R 2019).

*원 출처 표기*: `Wahezi SE, Alexeev E, et al. PM R 2018;10(6):616-22. doi:10.1016/j.pmrj.2017.11.011. PMID 29174073`
