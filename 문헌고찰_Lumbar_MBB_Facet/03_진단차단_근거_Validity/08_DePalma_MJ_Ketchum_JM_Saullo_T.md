## 이 문헌에서 인용된 근거 (1건)

### [확실] DePalma 2011: in an unselected chronic LBP spine-centre population, facet joint pain accounted for 31%, behind discogenic pain at 42%; probability of facet pain rises with age until ~70.

Retrospective chart review, academic spine center; 170 cases from 156 patients undergoing diagnostic procedures (provocation discography, dual comparative MBB, dual comparative SIJ blocks). Prevalence: internal disc disruption 42% (41.8%), facet joint pain 31%, sacroiliac joint pain 18% (18.2%). Patients with IDD were significantly younger than those with FJP or SIJP; increasing age was associated with decreased probability of IDD and increased probabilities of FJP and SIJP until approximately age 70.
