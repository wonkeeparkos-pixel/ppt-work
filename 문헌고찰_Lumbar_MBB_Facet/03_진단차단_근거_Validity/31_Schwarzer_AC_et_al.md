## 이 문헌에서 인용된 근거 (1건)

### [확실] No clinical feature — history or physical exam — predicts a positive confirmatory block; this is the reason a block is required at all.

In the 176-patient Schwarzer cohort, none of the clinical features tested was associated with response to the confirmatory block, and the Fairbank et al. and Helbig & Lee criteria were shown to be unreliable in distinguishing zygapophysial-joint pain from pain of other origin. A later systematic review of history and physical examination for diagnosing facet-origin chronic LBP reached a consistent conclusion (PMID 27723170, 2016). This underpins the whole diagnostic-block enterprise: there is no bedside substitute.
