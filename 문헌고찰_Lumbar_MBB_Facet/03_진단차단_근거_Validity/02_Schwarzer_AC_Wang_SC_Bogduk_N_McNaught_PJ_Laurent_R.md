## 이 문헌에서 인용된 근거 (1건)

### [확실] Schwarzer's Australian placebo-controlled intra-articular series gives a much higher prevalence: 40% (95% CI 27-53%).

63 patients with chronic LBP enrolled, 57 completed. Detailed history/exam plus intra-articular zygapophysial joint injections of 0.5% bupivacaine, with normal saline injected into paraspinal muscles as the control. 23/57 (40%; 95% CI 27-53%) failed to obtain relief after saline but obtained relief after one or more intra-articular local anaesthetic injections. Note the control was intramuscular saline, not intra-articular saline — a weaker placebo control than a true triple-block design; also a different (Australian rheumatology-referral) population than the Spine/Pain 1994 cohort, which is the usual explanation offered for 40% vs 15%.
