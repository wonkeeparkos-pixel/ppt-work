## 이 문헌에서 인용된 근거 (1건)

### [보통] Cohen's randomized cost-effectiveness trial is the empirical core of the 'zero blocks' argument: proceeding straight to RFA was most cost-effective.

Multicenter randomized comparative cost-effectiveness study, 151 subjects with suspected lumbar facetogenic pain, randomized to 0, 1, or 2 diagnostic medial branch blocks before lumbar facet RFA. Of 51 patients who received RF denervation with NO diagnostic block, 17 (33%) obtained a successful outcome. Of 16 patients who had a single diagnostic block and then proceeded to RF denervation, 8/16 (50%) were successful. Conclusion: using current reimbursement scales, proceeding to radiofrequency denervation WITHOUT a diagnostic block is the most cost-effective treatment paradigm. The mechanism is arithmetic — blocks raise the per-treated-patient success rate but the screening costs and screen-out losses exceed the gain. I could NOT verify the frequently quoted per-successful-treatment cost figures (~$6,286 / $17,142 / $15,241 for 0 / 1 / 2 blocks); treat those as unconfirmed.
