## 이 문헌에서 인용된 근거 (1건)

### [보통] The anatomic case against IA injection as a diagnostic test: joint capacity is only 1-2 mL, above which capsular rupture produces non-specific spread.

The lumbar facet joint holds a maximum of 1-2 mL of injected fluid; above that, rupture of the joint capsule occurs with extravasation into the paraspinal soft tissues and the epidural space, producing non-specific effects. Recommended IA injectate volume is therefore 1 to 1.5 mL. The diagnostic consequence: an IA injection that ruptures the capsule is no longer a test of the joint.
