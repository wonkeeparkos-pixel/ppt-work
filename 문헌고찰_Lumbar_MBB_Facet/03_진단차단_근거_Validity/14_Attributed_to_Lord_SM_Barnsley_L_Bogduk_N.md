## 이 문헌에서 인용된 근거 (1건)

### [미확인] Placebo-controlled (triple) blocks are the true reference standard; comparative blocks are the accepted practical surrogate, with imperfect specificity — the classic 'sensitivity 100%, specificity ~65%' figure could not be verified in this session.

The literature convention: a placebo-controlled block paradigm (local anesthetic, local anesthetic, and saline in random order, patient blinded) is the only design that controls placebo response, and is the reference standard against which comparative blocks are judged. The frequently quoted validation figure — that comparative local anesthetic blocks have ~100% sensitivity but only ~65% specificity relative to placebo-controlled blocks, derived from CERVICAL zygapophysial joint data (Lord SM, Barnsley L, Bogduk N, Clin J Pain 1995) — is stated here from recall and was NOT verified by any source retrieved in this session. Do not put the 65% on a slide without pulling the primary paper.
