### [보통] Dreyfuss 1997 CT study: 0.5 mL of injectate at the lumbar medial branch target reached the epidural space and/or intervertebral foramen in 16% of injections.

CT-based specificity study of lumbar medial branch and L5 dorsal ramus blocks in healthy volunteers using 0.5 mL contrast; contrast spread to the epidural space and intervertebral foramina was identified in 16% of injections. This is the canonical citation for 'more than half a millilitre stops being a target-specific test.' Full text not accessible this session; the 16% figure is reported consistently in secondary sources.
