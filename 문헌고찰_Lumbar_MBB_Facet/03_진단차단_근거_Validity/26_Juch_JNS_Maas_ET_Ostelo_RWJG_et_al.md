## 이 문헌에서 인용된 근거 (1건)

### [확실] The MINT trials found radiofrequency denervation added no clinically important benefit — and became the focal point of the selection-criteria debate.

Three pragmatic, multicenter, NON-BLINDED randomized clinical trials in 16 multidisciplinary pain clinics in the Netherlands. Mean difference in pain intensity between RF denervation and control at 3 months: facet joint trial -0.18 (95% CI -0.76 to 0.40); sacroiliac joint trial -0.71 (95% CI -1.35 to -0.06); combination trial -0.99 (95% CI -1.73 to -0.25). Conclusion: RF denervation combined with a standardized exercise program produced either no improvement or no clinically important improvement over exercise alone. The facet trial's point estimate did not even reach statistical significance.
