### [확실] Post-surgical (post-fusion/post-decompression) low back pain: facet prevalence drops to 16%, but single-block false-positive rate rises to 49%.

117 consecutive patients with chronic nonspecific LBP after lumbar surgical intervention(s), evaluated with controlled comparative local anesthetic blocks. Prevalence of lumbar facet joint pain 16%; false-positive rate with a single lidocaine block 49%. Conclusion: facet joints are a small but real pain generator in the post-surgical population — the key teaching point is the near 1-in-2 false-positive rate in this group.
