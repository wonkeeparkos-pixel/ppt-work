## 이 문헌에서 인용된 근거 (1건)

### [보통] The MINT rebuttal literature is substantial and centers on patient selection (single block, 50% cut-off) and technique.

Published critiques include Kapural L et al., 'RE: Juch JNS, et al...' Neuromodulation 2017 (PMID 29220124, doi:10.1111/ner.12729), and 'Evidence versus advocacy, as related to radiofrequency denervation in the treatment of chronic low back pain and the MINT trials' (2019, PMID 31831629). The recurring methodological objections are: selection via a single diagnostic block with a 50% relief threshold rather than dual blocks at 80%; non-blinded pragmatic design; questions about electrode placement/lesion technique; and co-intervention with exercise diluting the signal. I could not open these to extract their specific counter-arguments verbatim.
